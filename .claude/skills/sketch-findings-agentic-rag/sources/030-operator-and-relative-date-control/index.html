030 · Relative-Date Control

The filter choices must fit the field you pick, and the date control must make “expiring within 90 days” / “older than 90 days” obvious — while being honest that the window updates automatically (the dates shift forward as time passes) and that “within next…” leaves out already-overdue items. “Today” is pinned to Jun 19, 2026 here.

The available choices change to fit the field you pick — choose a field, then choose how to match it.

A · The operator itself encodes the direction

One date operator dropdown carries the full vocabulary including the two relative entries. Pick “within next…” and a [N] [unit] stepper appears. Fewest moving parts; the menu does the explaining.

The operator menu stays short — just “Date is…”. A segmented control picks the mode; Relative reveals a direction toggle + [N] [unit]. Keeps the field→operator menu uniform with string/number fields; pushes the date complexity into the value control.

C · Preset chips for the headline cases + Custom

The real use-cases are few: “expiring soon”, “stale”, “this year”. Offer them as one-tap chips; Custom… always expands to the full direction + N + unit. Fastest for the 80% case, no menu hunting.
