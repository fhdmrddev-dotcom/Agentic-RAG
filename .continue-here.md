## Decisions that must not be re-litigated

- **Width:** form-shaped pages are constrained (Settings/Connections 1152); **data-dense pages are full
  width** (Skills, Chat, Library). The MANIFEST's old *"1152 is THE measure"* entry is corrected in place.
  `231/drive.cjs` asserts the sketch declares no width token at all.
- **`added` ≠ done.** Terminal outcomes stay exactly three (`added`/`here`/`refused` — SC#5 needs no fourth
  destination); the queue adds a **journey**: *Waiting → Reading → **Readable***. The confirm says
  **Accepted**.
- **`INGEST_MAX_CONCURRENT_JOBS = 5`** (operator set). ⚠ It is **per uvicorn worker** — with
  `WORKER_COUNT=2` that is 10 actual, against a `postgres_pool_max` of 10 per worker.
- **`None` ≠ `[]`** for `only_external_ids`: `None` = everything, `[]` = nothing.

## Traps this session hit — do not repeat

1. ⛔ **A hidden duplicate of an interactive control is not a preserved contract, it is a second control.**
   Keeping a hidden `TabsList` "for the shell hook" produced a second `role="tab"` and broke 41 cases.
2. ⛔ **A badge may decorate a control's name; it may not RENAME it.** An un-hidden count badge changed a
   tab's accessible name and broke six `getByRole` lookups.
3. ⛔ **String-replace edits must assert they applied.** `only_external_ids` was written once against a
   reflowed docstring, matched nothing, and surfaced as a runtime 502.

   `GSD_VITEST_MAX_WORKERS=2 node scripts/vitest-count-gate.cjs` from the repo root.
