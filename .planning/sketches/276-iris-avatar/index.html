Decision 1 · one avatar or two on tool turns

Today a tool turn shows two avatars that pulse together: the MessageItem gutter avatar and the RunCard header's provider logo. run-state-honesty.md D5/D6 asks for one live avatar.

Decision 5 · which overlapping indicators go

Once the avatar moves, three other indicators repeat the same signal. The words stay, because they say what the agent is doing. The extra motion goes, because the avatar already says that it is working. ⚠ On a long turn the avatar scrolls out of view and the RunCard header stays sticky. The header keeps its ticking timer and shimmer bar, so the run never looks dead.

Decision 4 · light theme

The mark's core is #F2F4FE and its petals are pale lavender. On a white background the core disappears and the petals wash out. No light variant of the logo exists (docs/brand/README.md).
