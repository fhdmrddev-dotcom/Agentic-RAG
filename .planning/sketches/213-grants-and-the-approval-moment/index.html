Sketch 213 — Per-Tool Grants and the Approval Moment

Re-expresses the Stitch pass (213-stitch-grants/) against the tokens and rhythm that actually ship. Every colour below is copied from index.css .dark; every string comes from COPY.js. Two deliberate departures from Stitch, both argued in the CSS: the override edge is one colour meaning “you decided this” rather than a per-state colour, and the panel is clamp(480px,38%,640px) rather than a fixed 400.
