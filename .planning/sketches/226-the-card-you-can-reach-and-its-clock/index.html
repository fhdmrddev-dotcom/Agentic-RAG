The claim

  • Unmissable by construction. While a decision is pending it is not content, it is state — so it leaves the scrollback and occupies the one region always on screen.
  • Precedent exists: panel-shell.md's calm-loud interrupt — a blocked loop is made unmissable structurally rather than by shouting. The composer already locks during a pause.

The cost

  • The transcript loses the moment. On reload the decision has to resolve back into the message list, or the record has a hole — the same reload seam SeamCard exists for.
  • ⚠ Two pending approvals would stack over the composer and eat the column. Rare, but the design has to say what it does.

The claim

  • Reuses a pattern already shipped. Sketch 015-C's never-vanishes strip + "Jump to live" chip is exactly this shape, already built for the run status.
  • The transcript keeps the moment in place, so no reload seam is needed — the card is where it happened.
  • ⭐ The chip carries the clock. The time is never off screen even when the card is.

The cost

  • It is a pointer, not the decision. You still have to travel to act — one extra step at the exact moment the clock matters most.
  • ⚠ If the person is at the bottom composing when it arrives, the chip is the only signal, and it is small.

The claim — consistency, not a new idea

  • ⭐ The app already has this home. panel/PendingAskCard.tsx hosts a pending ask_user, and the panel section is literally headed "Needs you". Two kinds of "the agent is waiting on you" living in two different places is the drift.
  • The chat pointer + panel card is the shipped seam (sketch 007-C), not an invention.

The cost — and it is the big one

  • The panel collapses to a 52px rail. If it is closed when the approval arrives, the decision is behind a click and the clock is running. That is strictly worse than today unless arrival force-opens the panel.
  • At the measured 300px floor the card is cramped — "Always allow" barely fits, and it is the button whose consequence most needs room to be read.
  • ⚠ WorkspacePanel has no mount in any workflow page, so this lands in chat only.
