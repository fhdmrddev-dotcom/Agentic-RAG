is mounted beside it.

   "Everything you add here is searchable by the agent."

   inside the outer tab body. The Wave 1 `DocumentUpload` mount is relocated under the
   `add-files` `TabsContent` with `variant="hero"`. `UploadFolderPicker` is mounted
   beside it. The existing queue and Needs-attention markup stay under their respective
   sub-tabs. History is a placeholder shell for Plan 04.
