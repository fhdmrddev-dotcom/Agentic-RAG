**The replacement — three falsifiable properties, not a vibe:**

   *"This view has no screen: `{activeView}`."*

     <UnknownViewFallback view={activeView satisfies never} />

   developer error at `tsc`. `tsc --noEmit` alone checks ZERO files here — verify with
