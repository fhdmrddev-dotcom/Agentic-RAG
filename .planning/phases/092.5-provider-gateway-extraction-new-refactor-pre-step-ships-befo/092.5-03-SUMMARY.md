## Decisions Made
- See `key-decisions` frontmatter. The load-bearing one: adapters return the **bare** `stream_*` SYNC generator, NOT an async wrapper — because `stream_anthropic`/`stream_google` are sync generators driven by the consumer's threadpool drain (`for chunk in stream:`) with `close_fn=stream.close`. Any async re-wrap would have changed the drain/close machinery — a non-verbatim behavior change. The seam test was adapted to sync-iterate to match (Plan 01's async-generator fakes were a scaffold assumption; the real boundary is sync).
