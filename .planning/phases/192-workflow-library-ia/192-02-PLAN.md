<objective>
Ship D-04: project ownership onto the two read endpoints that feed the workflow library, as two
**computed, defaulted booleans** — `is_mine` and `is_system_global` — and widen the frontend wire type
to match.

Purpose: `PublishedWorkflow` carries `{id, slug, name, definition}` and **no ownership field**
(`frontend/src/lib/api.ts:1349`). Five of the six D-03 chips derive client-side from `definition`;
*Yours* cannot. Today it is a server round-trip (`?scope=mine`), and a re-query chip cannot deliver
157-B's promise of six chips with honest **simultaneous** counts. RESEARCH measured that neither
`created_by` nor `is_system_global` is in any SELECT list today — so this is "add two columns to three
SELECTs and two Pydantic fields", not "un-drop a field at serialization".

Output: two projected columns, two defaulted model fields computed in both handlers, a backend unit
test with a negative fence, and the widened TypeScript type.

**This plan is why "192 is frontend-only" is false** — Phase 184 shipped on that assumption and
verification inherited it (D-04 records this explicitly).
</objective>
