**Why `getBoundingClientRect` reads 419 and not 248, stated rather than left to look wrong:** the
React Flow viewport applies a zoom transform (~1.69×) to the whole graph, which scales the rendered
box but not the computed CSS. 248 × 1.69 ≈ 419. The computed `width` is the CSS truth; the rect is
the zoomed paint. The same factor explains the icon well measuring 104.8px for a `62px` source in
A3 (62 × 1.69 ≈ 104.8), which is a consistency check across two independent rows rather than a
coincidence.

confirmation, in your own words"*. **That half was not performed.** No screenshot was taken (the
