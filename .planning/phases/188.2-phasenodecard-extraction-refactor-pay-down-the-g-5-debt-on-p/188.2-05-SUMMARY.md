## Decisions Made

1. **The design prose stayed in its JSX comments.** See Deviations, Rule 3 — the plan asks both for the ring/chip/well prose to be lifted into each module's second docblock AND for the five spans to `diff` EMPTY, and only one of those is achievable. 2. **The signature-destructure divergence, stated explicitly so a reviewer does not read it as drift.** All three new modules destructure IN THE SIGNATURE, following `PlaneEditingLayer.tsx:110-121`. `PhaseNodeCard` itself takes `props` and destructures in the BODY (`:378`, `:393-408`) — not a house-style difference but a consequence of the 14-line comment that sits between its signature and its destructure. The new modules have no such comment, so they follow the container analog.
3. **`verdict` is typed `VerdictMarkKind`, not `NodeVerdictMark`.** Identical types; taking it from `nodePresentation` (where the table it indexes lives) keeps the import list at four and adds no dependency on the contract module. Fence N2's 184-08 narrowing — anchored on the two TINT identifiers rather than on the module path — is what makes importing from `nodePresentation` legal here, and it transferred unchanged.
4. **All five `RING_*` constants stay module-private**, as they were in the card. `RING_CIRCUMFERENCE` is consumed only by the `arc` derivation that moved with them, so no export was needed and none was added — the tests read `?raw` source and rendered DOM.
5. **The two JSX pairs were extracted as single spans** (`:567-656` and `:658-762`) rather than as the plan's four ranges, because `:600` and `:740` are blank separators. One span makes the `diff` exactly empty and guarantees the verdict-above-seal order `sealJsxBlock` depends on.
6. **Line endings normalised to CRLF after script assembly** (`sed -i 's/$/\r/'` then `git add`), the environment note `188.2-04` carried forward. The blob is byte-identical either way; this only keeps the working tree consistent with its 66 siblings.
