- **Commit:** `851005d7d`

**4. [Rule 1 - Design] Clamp on Save and blur rather than on change**
- RerankEngineBlock clamps on every keystroke. With a 2..50 bound that would turn a typed `25` into `2`→`25` and a typed `5` into `2`. The leaf keeps a string draft instead, and clamps when the value is sent and when the field loses focus. A cleared field drops out of the payload and returns to the loaded value on blur.
