Collapse the Builder's three stacked header bands into one row when `visual_workflow_canvas`
is on, reclaiming ~90px for the canvas — and leave the flag-off surface provably untouched.

Operator-reported in Phase 184 UAT: *"the canvas space is very narrow because the header
above is taking too much space… especially with the smaller screens."* Measured: 275px of
chrome above a 288px canvas on a 639px viewport — the flow got 45% of the screen.

Full analysis, measurements and the three costed options:
`.planning/phases/184.1-builder-header-consolidation/184.1-CONTEXT.md` (Option A selected).
</objective>

<constraints>
**THE FLAG GATE IS THE WHOLE POINT — do not skip it as an optimisation.**

All three target bands render regardless of the canvas flag, so a naive merge changes the
flag-off surface D-181-01 requires stay byte-identical for everyone, operators included.

And that promise is currently UNTESTED for this surface: `revertByteIdentical.test.tsx`
pins `NAV_ITEMS` and the Control-Room radio only; `WorkflowBuilderPage.canvas.test.tsx`
pins flag-off *behaviours* (no editing affordance, `rails` absent, panel dismissal,
empty-draft trigger) but nothing pins the header's structure or height. A regression here
would ship green. Gate on the flag and flag-off identity holds by construction.

**Do NOT move logic between components.** The merged row owns LAYOUT only. Each control
keeps its current owner, handlers and state. This is a re-flow.

**Do NOT remove anything.** Every control, badge and label survives.
</constraints>
