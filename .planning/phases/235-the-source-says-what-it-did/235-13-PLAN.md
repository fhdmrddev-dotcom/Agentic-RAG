it. Give it a NAME, a sentence and its own control — and, in the same plan, give the vocabulary leaf every other
word this closure round needs, so the two render plans in wave 2 consume a settled leaf instead of racing it.

but `watch_service.py` seam 2 writes `failure_cause=None`, so after three paused ticks the surface says
*"It stopped, and no reason was recorded."* and offers **Retry now** — on a state somebody chose deliberately,
with a control that provably cannot change it. That is SC#2's *"offers ONE control that fixes it"*, failing.

Output: a five-member `Cause` union whose fifth member is written by exactly one seam and inferred by nothing;
the matching sentence and control rows on the frontend; and the four new vocabulary exports wave 2 needs.
