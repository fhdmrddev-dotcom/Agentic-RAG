**Declined — `D-200.1-03-A`, the one deviation:** the sheet's `#464651`. On the ground both
drawings share it measures ~2.1:1 — below even the 3:1 *graphical* floor — where the shipped
`text-muted-foreground/60` composites to ~3.4:1. Adopting the literal would make the clock column
materially harder to read **while looking like fidelity**. The sheet's value is quoted in the
component's docblock so the declined value stays findable, and is rendered by nothing (asserted,
with a positive control proving it IS present in the declining comment).

## Deviations from Plan

### `[Rule 1 - Bug]` The plan's `<token_mapping>` block was re-derived, and three of its four figures moved

- **Found during:** Task 1 (the block itself instructs *"VERIFY each … do not trust this block on
  its own"*).
- **Issue:** converting the tokens by hand (sRGB → relative luminance → WCAG 2.x contrast) does
  not reproduce three of the four published figures.

  | | plan's block | **re-derived** |
  |---|---|---|
  | `--background` 216 45% 4% | `#060A0F` — *"byte-for-byte"* the sheet's ground | **`#06090F`** — one step off in GREEN |
  | `--primary` 239 100% 82% | `#A3A5FF`, 8.87:1 | **`#A3A5FF` ✓ CONFIRMED**, 8.91:1 on our ground (8.87:1 on the sheet's) |
  | sheet `#464651` | 2.16:1 | **2.14:1** on ours (2.13:1 on the sheet's) |
  | `text-muted-foreground/60` | `#5D6572`, 3.43:1 | **`#5D6472`**, **3.35:1** on ours (3.37:1 on the sheet's) |

  The `#5D6572` / 3.43 error is *downstream* of the `#060A0F` one — compositing 60% over a green
  channel of 10 instead of 9 moves the result by exactly one step.
- **Fix:** both sets are recorded in the docblock — the plan's **beside** the measured ones, never
  over them — because a measurement quietly replaced reads as though it had never been wrong. This
  also satisfies the plan's acceptance criterion literally (the docblock still contains the literals
  `#464651`, `2.16` and `3.43`, all greppable).
- **⚠ EVERY VERDICT SURVIVES THE CORRECTION**, which is why the decision stands unchanged: the
  declined literal is still under the 3:1 graphical floor, the kept value is still materially
  better, and the indigo is still an exact token match. What is withdrawn is only the claim that
  the two grounds are *byte-for-byte* identical — they are near-identical, not equal.
- **Files:** `RunTranscript.tsx` · **Commit:** `c0c06aeb`
