<objective>
SC#3, the half nobody mapped. Migration 119 and the backend writers make the interrupted
`workflow_phases` row read `cancelled` **in the database**. This plan makes the client say so.

⚠ **THIS GAP IS NOT IN RESEARCH.md OR PATTERNS.md, AND IT IS RECORDED HERE RATHER THAN SILENTLY
CLOSED.** Both documents mapped the stopped-state vocabulary to `RunCard.statusGlyph`/`statusWord`
only. Measured at HEAD, the panel spine's status vocabulary is a **different, closed union**:
`Phase["status"]` (`types/index.ts:1035-1043`) has eight members and no `cancelled`, and
`phaseStatusFromDb` (`lib/phaseState.ts`) is TOTAL over every string, resolving anything it does
not recognise to `unknown`. **So without this plan, a stopped run's interrupted phase renders
"Unknown" on the panel spine** — fail-CLOSED and therefore not a lie, but not D-13's *"the
interrupted phase reads stopped, not failed"* either, and it fails G-4 lived-experience row 1.

Purpose: the widening **is the mechanism, not a side effect**. Every downstream table is declared
as an exhaustive `Record<…>` precisely so that an added member is a TYPECHECK ERROR at each of them
until someone states what the new state looks like. That is Phase 188's and Phase 189's own
recorded design and this plan uses it exactly as intended.

Output: one new member on each of two unions, and one stated row at every consumer the compiler
names — plus the one consumer the compiler **cannot** name.
</objective>
