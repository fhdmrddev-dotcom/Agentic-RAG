## The standing lesson this file exists to obey

**A fence that cannot fire is worse than no fence.** Phases 190, 192.1 and 193.1 each registered
fences later found to be sweeping the empty string, asserting the absence of a *field* where a *set
member* had been added, or scoped to a renamed module. In 193.1 **four** such fences were caught —
every one of them by *planting*, none by reading.

⇒ **Every fence in this phase carries a named plant, and the plant must be observed RED before the
fix lands.** A plan that reports a green fence without having seen it red has not validated anything.

⚠ **THE LESSON LANDED AGAIN, IN SIX OF THIS PHASE'S EIGHT PLANS, AND IS RECORDED HERE AT THE TOP
RATHER THAN LEFT IN THE MAP.** Plans 02, 03, 04, 05, 06 and 07 each found at least one fence that
could not fire, or that fired for the wrong reason. **Every one was caught by RUNNING, none by
reading.** The five distinct shapes, because the shapes generalise and the individual fences do not:

1. **A raw sweep reds on the PROSE DOCUMENTING the thing it sweeps for.** Five instances in one
   phase: `194.1-02`'s pure-read fence red on the COLUMN NAME `updated_at` (which uppercases to
   `UPDATED_AT`, containing `UPDATE`); `194.1-03`'s `setTimeout` fence red on the comment stating the
   rule; `194.1-04`'s docblock red on **four** separate fences including a shipped one it does not
   own; `194.1-06`'s two greps both red on the docblock explaining why those very identifiers are
   forbidden. ⚠ **The remedy differed each time, and "strip the comments" was WRONG once** — the
   shipped `WorkspacePanel.test.tsx` **F-1 / V-05** union fence sweeps RAW and un-stripped *on
   purpose*, so the prose was the violation, not the fence.
2. **A fence that passes VACUOUSLY.** `194.1-05`'s first dispatchability assertion rendered a retired
   card cold and asserted `disabled === true` — green against a component with **no retirement arm at
   all**, because `canSubmit` requires an answer and an unanswered card is already disabled.
3. **A fence that reds for a reason other than its subject.** `194.1-07`'s `expectBandSentence`
   **sampled** where it had to **wait**; under plant P1 it red on a band COUNT (an announcement race)
   rather than on the gate the plant was aimed at. **Had it stopped there, P1 would have been recorded
   as catching a gate defect it had not caught.**
4. **A fence scoped one line too narrowly to see the shape it guards.** `194.1-03`'s L-01 source arm
   asked whether ONE line named both the stopping slice and `runStatus`; the only shape that
   suppression takes spans five lines, so it stayed GREEN under its own plant. Widened to an
   eight-line window and re-driven RED.
5. ⚠ **A pin in an UNGATED suite is a pin nothing checks.** Plan 01's `toBe(1047)` line pin was
   broken by plan 06 and was **already RED at plan 07's base with nobody able to see it**, because
   `src/components/chat` has no `TARGETS` entry. *If a phase pins a figure, pin it where the gate
   runs — or do not pin it.*
