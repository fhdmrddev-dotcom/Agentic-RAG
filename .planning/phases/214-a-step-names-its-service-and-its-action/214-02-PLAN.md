<objective>
Carry the failure reason and the step's service/action identity from the `workflow_phases` row to
**both** run wire models, through the one shared unwrap, and widen the two client types to match.

Purpose: `BUG-260826-05`'s mechanism is measured and it is neither the emitter nor the executor
(D-214-23). The reason **is** written — `db/workflows.py:1835` puts it at `output._failure_reason`
on every `fail_phase` — and **nothing projects it**: `grep -rn "_failure_reason" frontend/src`
returns zero, so `PhaseCard.classifyFailure` reads `phase.error`, which `types/index.ts:188`
documents as live-SSE-only, finds it empty on any reconciled run, and fires `reason_unknown` **while
the reason sits in the row**. That is D-v2.5-03 exactly.

⚠ **And 38 of 43 failed rows store `output` as a jsonb STRING SCALAR**, so a reader written as
`output["_failure_reason"]` finds it on 2 of 43. The parse is the load-bearing half.

⚠ **AND THE WIRE HAS FIVE HOPS, NOT THREE.** The mechanical seam derivation (recorded in plan
`214-14`) walked `_failure_reason` from its writer to its readers and found the two hops that no plan
owned: the **client mirror** `frontend/src/lib/api/threads.ts::WorkflowPhaseState`, and
`StreamsProvider.tsx::reconcilePhases`, which constructs every `Phase` **field-by-field**. That is
exactly the incident the mirror's own comment at `threads.ts:1247-1279` records against Phase 200-02:
*"`200-02` widened the BACKEND `WorkflowPhaseState` … and did NOT widen this client mirror, so
`GET /threads/{id}/workflow` has been sending them and the panel has been unable to declare them."*
**This plan owns all five hops so that cannot happen twice on the same file.**

Output: one projection, two widened wire models **populated by both of their builders**, THREE widened
client types, both `reconcilePhases` branches mapped, and a suite driven RED against a string-scalar row.
