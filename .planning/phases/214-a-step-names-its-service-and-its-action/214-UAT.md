## 4 · What a verdict may say

- **✅ pass** — driven, with the observation recorded, not inferred.
- **⚠ partial** — driven, and something specific was wrong. **Name it.**
- **⛔ blocked** — could not be driven. **Name the reason and the blocking id.** A missing provider key,
  a missing connection row and a known defect are all legitimate blocks; *"did not get to it"* is
  recorded as **not run**, which is a different word and must not be written as a pass.
- **not run** — honest, and a legitimate way to close a phase **as a decision**. Record the owed rows
  in the ROADMAP progress row and in `STATE.md`, and name which row to run first.

⚠ **Closing this phase with owed rows is legitimate. Claiming they ran is not.**

⚠ **A UAT fix needs its own UAT row.** A defect found here and fixed is re-driven, in a new row, with
its own verdict — a fix verified only by the test written for it has been verified by its author.
