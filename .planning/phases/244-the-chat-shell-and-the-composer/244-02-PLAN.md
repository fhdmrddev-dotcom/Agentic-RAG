**⛔ THE `.pdf` RULING (D-244-24) — MADE, not discovered: `.pdf` IS TAKEN.**

D-244-24 says *"Assuming either way is the failure"*. The ruling and its three reasons:

1. The sketch's own finding: *"a signed contract PDF is the likeliest first thing anyone attaches."*
   Refusing it makes criterion 4 fail on the most probable first use of the feature.
2. Task 2 of this plan gives the sandbox reach to attachments, and `pypdf` is already in
   `agentic-rag-sandbox:101.1` (`docs/SANDBOX-PACKAGES.md`). So a `.pdf` becomes **genuinely
   usable**, not merely accepted — which is what makes the cost worth paying rather than a
   bigger promise.
3. The cost is bounded and named: one `_PDF_EXT` category plus a magic-byte branch mirroring
   `_image_magic_ok`, plus the three `accept=`/COPY copies — which Task 1 collapses to one fenced
   source anyway.

`_looks_like_text`. Adding it bare to `_ALLOWED_EXT` is also wrong — it reaches the unreachable
belt-and-braces 422 at `workspace.py:217`. It needs its own category and its own validator.
