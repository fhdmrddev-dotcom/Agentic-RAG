<objective>
Make it impossible for a field the operator never touched to take the whole Search tab down — and
make the one refusal that survives say what is actually wrong.

Purpose: `SettingsPage.tsx:873-899` sends **all 22 Search fields unconditionally** on every save. So
one stored value out of range refuses the entire tab, including the reranker, the embedding model,
the retrieval threshold, `rrf_k` — and, since Phase 241 D-09, `hnsw_ef_search`, which is the knob
Phase 246's whole remedy is delivered on. The operator sees *"Images read per document must be
between 1 and 1000"* on a **Search** tab where they changed a threshold, pointing at a field they
never edited, on a value they never typed.

Two changes, in that causal order: the payload stops carrying what did not change, and the refusal
that remains reachable names the stored value as the cause.

Output: a diffed payload with a silent-drop fence over it; one shared refusal helper at all four
bound sites; three SettingsPage suites gated where one was.
</objective>
