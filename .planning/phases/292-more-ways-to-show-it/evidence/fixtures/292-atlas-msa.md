## Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice. Either party
may terminate immediately for a material breach that is not cured within 30 days of written notice.

## Liability

Each party's total liability under this agreement is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before
the claim. The cap does not apply to death, personal injury, fraud or breach of confidentiality.

notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current term. The current term ends on
15 March 2027, so the renewal notice deadline is 15 December 2026.
