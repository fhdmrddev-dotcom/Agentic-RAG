<objective>
Item 3 of Phase 206.1 — **the per-service mark map**. Replace `ConnectionsTab.tsx`'s three
invented `fluent-emoji` capability glyphs with ONE shared module that gives every connection its
own service's real mark, gives MCP its own real mark, and gives the two vendorless shapes (SMTP,
unknown) an honest neutral one.

Purpose: the icon convention (Running Design Decision 43) says a service shows its OWN mark, from
one source, byte-identical everywhere. The ROADMAP makes that binding for this surface, and names
the failure modes precisely: *a logo is approximated, per-surface, or resolves EMPTY in production*,
and *the MCP row borrows another service's mark because it has no capability*.

This plan is FIRST in the phase for three measured reasons: it is the only item fully provable in
vitest, it is the only item that needs the npm install (which must happen ONCE, on the main working
tree), and **both other items render a mark**.

Output: `connectionMark.tsx` + its suite, the `@iconify-json/logos` devDependency, `ConnectionsTab.tsx`
rewired to the one render path, and two corrections-beside-their-originals.
</objective>
