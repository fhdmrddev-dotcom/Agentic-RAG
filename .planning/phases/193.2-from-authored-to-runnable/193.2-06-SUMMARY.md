## ⚠ THE `external_action` DISPLACEMENT QUESTION — ESTABLISHED, AND PLAN 05's PREMISE IS HALF FALSE

This was the most important inherited item, and the answer is stated plainly because it changes
what the copy is allowed to imply. Plan 05 flagged that its new authoring clause forbids
`llm_human_input`/`ask_user`, so the model may displace onto `external_action`, which "ALSO always
asks approval" and which `_interactive_phase_failures` does not refuse — and that if true, *"the
refusal copy you are writing describes a gate with a gap in it."*

**Measured at HEAD, from source, both halves:**

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `_interactive_phase_failures` does not refuse `external_action` | ✅ **TRUE** | The helper matches exactly two shapes: `config.phase_type == "llm_human_input"`, and a validator whose `on_failure == "ask_user"`. `grep external_action publish_service.py` → **one hit, and it is a comment** (`:845`, about the golden-run ctx). |
| …therefore the gate has a HOLE that could wedge a publish | ❌ **FALSE** | The consequence does not follow, for two independent shipped reasons. |

**Why the consequence does not follow — two mechanisms, neither of them this gate:**

1. **The armed action-risk checkpoint is AUTO-CONTINUED on a golden run.**
   `harness_engine.py:837` — `if _armed and getattr(ctx, "is_golden_run", False):` logs and
   continues, and its own comment says why it exists: before it, *"the engine could not tell a
   publish from a live run and burned `harness_publish_max_seconds` waiting for a person nobody
   had asked."* That is 189/D-19, and it is the fix for exactly the class plan 05 feared.
2. **The send itself is gated, and the golden run records rather than sends.** `phase_types.py`
   GATE 1 / D-16 (`:2194`), whose docblock states D-19 verbatim: *"the executor still owns no
   waiting … There is no `await` on a human anywhere below."*

**⚠ AND WIDENING THE GATE IS NOT MERELY OUT OF SCOPE — IT IS FORBIDDEN BY A SHIPPED FENCE.**
`test_publish_service.py::test_the_armed_checkpoint_is_not_a_validator` (`:898-936`) asserts
`_interactive_phase_failures(armed) == []` and carries this in its own docblock:

> ⚠ **THIS ASSERTION IS NOT AN OVERSIGHT — IT IS A DELIBERATE FENCE.** Extending
> `_interactive_phase_failures` to name armed phases is CONFLICT-1 **Option B**, which makes an
> `external_action` workflow UNPUBLISHABLE and therefore contradicts D-06 outright. It was
> REJECTED.

So the gate was not widened, and the plan's instruction not to widen it coincides with a
pre-existing hard refusal rather than merely with scope.

**⇒ WHAT THIS CONSTRAINS IN THE COPY, AND IT WAS HONOURED:** the gate is **not exhaustive over
"steps that involve a person"**, so no new literal may imply that it is. None does — every branch
speaks only about *this* workflow and *this* step ("**one step** stops to wait for a person"), and
not one asserts that no other step could ever involve a human. The non-exhaustiveness is also
recorded permanently in `_interactive_phase_failures`' own docstring, with the `external_action`
mechanism named, so the next reader inherits the measurement instead of re-deriving it.

⚠ **One inherited phrase is corrected rather than repeated.** `SEED-164`'s taxonomy row 3 says
`external_action` *"always asks approval, records rather than sends"*. That is **true of a LIVE
run and misleading if read as "wedges a publish"** — on the publish path the approval is
auto-continued and the send is skipped. Both readings are recorded here so the seed's sentence is
not later quoted as a publish-path claim. **Flagged for `193.2-08`'s k/N harness**, as the plan
asked.

`193.2-D10-D19-CONFIRMATION.md` §2: the pre-193.2 interactive message **105**,

clears it, and the case an author will actually see is **~150**. `_LABEL_MAX_CHARS = 72` sits mid

10 KB refusal.
