## Decisions Made

- **clearMessages() branch (a) chosen — delete, not guard.** D-068.5 CONTEXT explicitly offered planner discretion. Deletion is the smaller diff and the structural guarantee from `bucketsBySurface` per-thread keying (Phase 067.3) is the durable invariant — a runtime guard would just check `!== streamingThreadIdRef.current` which is the same predicate Branch D-3 already enforces inside `clearThreadBucket`. Two predicates with the same intent is duplicated invariant risk.
- **Sync hydrate inside factory closure, not useEffect.** Useffect-based hydrate would leave the first render with `new Map()` and trigger a re-render on hydrate completion — the exact "blank then content" flicker BUG-260513-01 is trying to close. Closure-time hydrate makes the initial state already-correct.
- **`lastAccessedAt` carried forward across writes.** Newly-written threads still get a fresh timestamp, but threads that survived the prior write keep their existing `lastAccessedAt`. Without this, every write would refresh every thread's stamp and LRU would degrade into FIFO.
