<objective>
Close the loop: approve a proposal, re-eval the draft, gate promotion honestly. POST approve INSERTs
the immutable `skill_versions(source='self_improve')` draft WITHOUT touching the live skill (D-05),
then launches a both-arms re-eval by REUSING the `start_eval_run` companion-run machinery + `run_eval_job`
on the SOURCE run's provider/model (D-11/D-12) with the `skill_instructions_override` seam (Plan 02) so
the WITH arm measures the DRAFT. When the re-eval terminates, `promotion_gate()` (D-13, case-matched
no-regression + improvement) decides: PASS -> promote (UPDATE `skills.instructions`, accepting the 132
trigger's benign `manual` duplicate per Pitfall #2) + status='promoted'; FAIL -> status='not_promoted'
with honest counts (force-promotable, D-06); orphaned/stale -> reconcile to status='interrupted' + a
re-run affordance (D-14).

This plan WRITES (does not declare) the D-13 honest counts onto the locked `SkillProposalResponse.gate`
field (a `PromotionGate` model owned by Plan 04's `eval_run.py`). The counts are recomputed from the two
runs whenever a proposal has a terminal re-eval — so they show on BOTH `promoted` AND `not_promoted`
states and survive reloads (D-13 "always displayed"), WITHOUT a schema change and WITHOUT this plan
touching `eval_run.py` (Plan 04 owns it; the 04-then-05 sequencing on `evals.py` is preserved).

Purpose: the auto-re-eval gate that makes "never auto-applies, human always in the loop" real.
Output: approve/rerun/force-promote routes + the gate + the reconcile/promote logic + gate-counts
population + tests.

Honors D-05, D-06, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-14; SC#10 (D-15 — UAT in VALIDATION.md); Pitfall #2.
</objective>
