<objective>
Ship the remedy as a product capability, not as a tuning constant.

Purpose: the collapse mechanism is concrete and measured — `hnsw.ef_search = 40` and
`hnsw.iterative_scan = off` on the server, `hybrid_candidate_count = 20` in config, and seven
predicates applied INSIDE the `ORDER BY … <=> … LIMIT` scan. The scan walks 40 global candidates,
discards with seven predicates, then takes 20. A migration-baked `SET hnsw.ef_search` inside the
function would need zero Python but could never be changed from the UI, which is exactly what the
operator asked for. Making it a setting costs a small landing on a hot file, and that trade is
taken deliberately.

Output: migration 176 (authored, not applied), two `config.py` defaults plus two hardcoded-only
companions, the `_val` mapping, the API's request/response/refusal surface, a new
`retrieval_tuning.py` holding the `SET LOCAL` helper, a three-line landing in
`retrieval_service.py`, and two controls on the shipped Retrieval card.

⛔ **`retrieval_service.py`'s G-5 extraction has been OWED since Phase 231 and is STILL OWED after
this plan.** This is the second milestone landing on it. The ROADMAP is explicit that a THIRD must
propose the extraction before adding behaviour. Nothing in this plan may be written or summarised
in a way that reads as a discharge.
