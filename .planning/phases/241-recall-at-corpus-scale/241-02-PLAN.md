<objective>
Build a corpus large enough and skewed enough for the collapse to be visible — on the operator's
local Docker Postgres, in a database that can be dropped, and never in the operator's real one.

Purpose: the live corpus is 7,953 chunks with tenant skew of roughly one third each — 241-CONTEXT
F-5 calls that *"precisely the shape SEED-076 says hides the defect"*. A tenant owning 0.2% of a
corpus starves `hnsw.ef_search = 40` regardless of whether the corpus is 250k chunks or 20M; the
mechanism is SELECTIVITY. This plan builds the smallest corpus at which that mechanism is
measurable, out of the project's own bootstrap artifact so the function under measurement is the
real one.

Output: `scripts/build-recall-bench.py` — build, skew, size-report and teardown — plus the unit
guard that it cannot be pointed at anything except a loopback `recall_bench`.

⚠ **This plan AUTHORS and SMOKE-PROVES the builder. The full-scale build is 241-04's task**, after
the operator has applied migration 176 and `supabase/full-schema.sql` has been regenerated (D-12:
the bench reads that artifact, so it must be current when the real corpus is built).
</objective>
