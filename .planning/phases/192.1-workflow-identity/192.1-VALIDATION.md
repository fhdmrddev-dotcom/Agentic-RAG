## ⚠ The gate's two knobs, and what is NOT covered

`TARGETS` (`vitest-count-gate.cjs:1251`) decides what **RUNS**; `BASELINE` (`:122`) decides what is
**PINNED**. A file can land outside both — that is the eighth recorded occurrence of this trap.

- ✅ `"src/components/workflows"` is a **directory** target, so **every new module under `library/`
  runs automatically** — no `TARGETS` edit needed.
- ✅ `"src/pages/WorkflowsPage.test.tsx"` is a file-level entry added by `192-01`.
- ❌ **`frontend/src/lib/api.workflows.test.ts` is outside BOTH knobs.** It references
  `createWorkflowDraft`. D-22 forbids touching that throw site — if a plan does anyway, it is
  **ungated**, and that must be said out loud rather than discovered.
