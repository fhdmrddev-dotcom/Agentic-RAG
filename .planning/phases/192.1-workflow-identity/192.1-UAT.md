## What shipped, in the operator's vocabulary

The **identity line** is a new second line on every workflow card, at DOM position 2 — under the
name, above the folder chip. It renders, in this order:

```
[Yours | Shared] · [discriminator] · [discriminator] [1 of N] · changed <when>
```

- **`Yours` / `Shared`** — whose it is.
- **discriminators** — computed per row from what actually differs among the rows sharing its name.
  **At most two, ever.** They are drawn from `Copy of <parent>` / `v3 of v2` / `Original`,
  the project, `Ready to run` / `Still building` / `Shared starter`, and `v3`.
- **`1 of N`** — appears **only** when the name collides. `N` counts the whole library, not the
  filtered view, so it does not change while you type.
- **`changed <when>`** — nine bands: `just now` · `N min ago` · `N hours ago` · `yesterday` ·
  `N days ago` · `last week` · `N weeks ago` · `last month` · `N months ago`.

A row with a **unique** name still carries the line, but carries **no discriminators** — lineage
only if it really is a fork, plus the ownership word and `changed`.

The **fork verb** (`Make my own copy`) now opens a prompt titled **`Name your copy`**, whose primary
button reads **`Create my copy`** — never `Confirm`.
