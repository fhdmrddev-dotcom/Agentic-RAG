<objective>
Close CR-03 and WR-09: put the carried paragraph under the same guard its sibling already has, and
make the sentence it renders true of both causes it covers.

Purpose: 187-16 fixed CR-01 — a sentence misattributing who applied a governance gate — and its own
fix shipped a NEW sentence with the same defect class and zero rendered coverage. `grep -rn
"seed-receipt-carried" frontend/src/` returns exactly one hit at HEAD (the component's own attribute,
re-measured this session), so the paragraph could be deleted, print the wrong number, or drift from
its formatter with all 452 tests green. Separately the sentence states "by its own settings" over a
count that includes `escalated`, whose own per-step reason two lines below reads "you turned this on
by hand".

## The WR-09 design choice, made in the open: shape (a), cause-agnostic — by DELETING the false clause

`seedReceiptCarriedLead` becomes `"1 step was already set to must prove it."` /
`"N steps were already set to must prove it."` — the current sentence with its attributive tail
removed. Nothing is added.

**Why (a) and not (b), the two-sentence split.** (b) is more honest at paragraph level but adds a
THIRD paragraph to a card sketch 150-B approved as a single-shot arrival, and it re-opens the count
arithmetic — `carriedCount` would split into `alreadySetCount` + `escalatedCount`, each owing its own
zero case and its own coverage. The per-step rows already carry the cause verbatim
(`seedReceiptStepReason("already-set")` → "it already has to cite its sources";
`seedReceiptStepReason("escalated")` → "you turned this on by hand"), and every row is already
asserted. Paragraph-level cause attribution is therefore redundant with a surface that is already
guarded — it buys nothing and costs the arrival composition.

**Why the DELETION and not the reviewer's suggested replacement.** `187-REVIEW.md` proposes
`"1 more step was already set to must prove it before I started."` Two measured objections:
1. **"more" is an antecedent word.** The carried paragraph renders ALONE on the typical non-KB draft
   (the `STRICT_EMIT_ONLY_PHASES` fixture), so "more" would point at a sentence that is not there.
   `definitionOps.ts`'s own docblock property 3 forbids exactly this, and `definitionOps.test.ts:866`
   partially operationalizes it with a lead-in-antecedent regex. **Measured caveat (plan-check
   round 3):** that regex is anchored to the string start and matches only
   `That|Those|The other|More|Another|Also`, so the reviewer's wording — which always leads with a
   digit — would NOT actually go RED against it. The regex does not enforce this objection; the
   docblock property does. This objection therefore rests on the declared property, not on an
   existing failing test, and objection 2 below is the independently sufficient reason.
2. **"before I started" is a first-person temporal claim that is not reliably true of `escalated`.**
   `grounding_escalated` is an ordinary `PhaseSpec` field and `generate_workflow_definition` neither
   strips nor re-derives it, so on a seeded draft the flag can arrive in the model's own emission —
   i.e. not before the generation started.

Deleting the clause claims nothing about *when* or *by what*. It states only what both carried causes
genuinely share — the step already wore the seal — and leaves *which* cause to the row that names it.

Output: a guarded, honest carried paragraph and a docblock that is true again.
