control for this failure mode. Scope-boundness is a structural property of the definition; it belongs
in `/validate`, where the answer is the same every time. This is SC#3's counterexample: safe-by-
construction cannot ship while an unbound retrieval workflow reaches a golden run unchallenged.

report frontmatter.
