<objective>
Stop the receipt telling a user they did something they may not have done, and make a fourth
grounding cause impossible to ship silently.

Purpose: round 3 deleted the paragraph's cause claim and recorded, in its own docblock, that naming
the cause is the ROW's job because the row is "the one place that is handed the cause". But the row's
sentence for `escalated` is not a statement about a cause — it is a statement about a person:
"you turned this on by hand". `grounding_escalated` is an ordinary `PhaseSpec` field and the
generator's emit tool schema is the whole `WorkflowDefinition` model, so a model emission carrying
that bit validates and reaches this card. The stronger of the two claims was left standing under a
docblock presenting the row as its safe home.

Beside it, the same function's `default:` arm swallows every future `GroundingCause` member and
returns the empty string, which the row renders as a face followed by a dangling em-dash and
nothing — on the one surface whose job is that a seal never arrives unexplained. The module already
establishes the right idiom 180 lines down and names it.

Output: one sentence that says what its bit knows, one exhaustiveness guard in the file's own shape,
and fences over both that carry positive controls.
</objective>
