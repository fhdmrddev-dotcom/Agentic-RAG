<objective>
Make the safety the AI silently applied legible, without implying an event the surface never received.

Purpose: SC#3 promises *"a seeded grounded node auto-gets its citation/confidence gate —
safe-by-construction."* In practice two steps arrive carrying a ⛨ seal the user never asked for and
nothing on the canvas explains why. Safety nobody can see is indistinguishable from magic, and magic is
not trust. The receipt names the step count and each auto-grounded step **with its reason**, states the
one-way rule plainly, dismisses, and ends by saying nothing is committed.

Generation is single-shot — one provider call on a valid first emit — so the whole canvas arrives in
one DOM batch. A staged, node-by-node reveal would be pacing dressed as progress.

Output: `SeedReceipt.tsx` and its suite.
</objective>
