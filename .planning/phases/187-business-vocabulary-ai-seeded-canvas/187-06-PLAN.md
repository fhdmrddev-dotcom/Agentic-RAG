<objective>
Make "armed ⇒ the person is asked before the body runs" a property of the phase, not a position in a list.

Purpose: today an author-declared `timing="pre"` / `on_failure="ask_user"` validator preempts the
armed gate. `run_gates` returns the FIRST failure (`validators.py:248`), the engine's armed branch is
keyed on a string-prefix sniff of the failing finding, and an `ask_user` Proceed falls through to the
body at `harness_engine.py:739` without re-running the remaining pre-gates. So the person is shown the
wrong choices, `action_risk_pending` is never emitted, and the step runs unapproved. This is SC#3's
counterexample: safe-by-construction cannot ship while a governance gate can be silently skipped.

Output: the hoisted checkpoint, the explicit `is_action_risk` parameter, the closed fail-open, and
`effective_phase` reduced to its `citations_required` append.
</objective>
