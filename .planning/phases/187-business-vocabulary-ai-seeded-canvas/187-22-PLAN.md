<objective>
Make the seed receipt describe the event it names.

Purpose: the card's copy is past-tense and first-person — "Here's what I built", "so I set them to
must prove it", "N steps were already set". Its data source is present-tense: `phases` is a live
`useStore` selector (`WorkflowBuilderPage.tsx:573`) handed straight into the card, and nothing
closes the card on an edit. `PhaseFormPanel` sits on the same screen and writes the exact field the
receipt's grounding classification reads. So ten seconds after the receipt arrives, the author can
switch a KB tool on and watch the card claim their own act as the AI's. That is CR-01's failure
shape for the third time, on the one surface whose entire purpose (SC#3) is that safety is
attributed to whoever actually applied it.

Two prior rounds fixed this class by editing what the copy SAYS. This one fixes where the copy READS
FROM, which is the only fix that closes all three inputs at once.

Output: a `receiptPhases` snapshot captured at generation time, a prop contract that says why, and
two fences — an end-to-end one that goes red today, and a standing one that forbids the tempting
wrong fix.
</objective>

## Read this before Task 1 — one claim in `187-REVIEW.md` does not survive checking

`187-REVIEW.md` proposes BOTH a caller-side fix (a `receiptPhases` snapshot in
`WorkflowBuilderPage.tsx`) AND a falsifying test that renders `SeedReceipt` directly and
`rerender`s it with an edited `phases` array, expecting the authorship claim to stay absent.

**Those two cannot both be right.** `SeedReceipt` is a pure leaf — every count and sentence is
recomputed from the `phases` PROP on every render (`SeedReceipt.tsx:193,208,212-213,216-217`). A
caller-side snapshot does not change that. Hand the component a mutated array directly and it will
recompute, correctly, forever. The review's proposed component test would therefore still fail after
the review's own proposed fix, and the only way to make it pass would be to give the leaf a hidden
cache of its first props — which contradicts the component's shipped contract, breaks a second
generation's receipt, and hides the caller bug rather than fixing it.

The resolution this plan implements, and which the executor must not quietly re-litigate:

- **The falsifying test lives at the PAGE level** (`WorkflowBuilderPage.canvas.test.tsx`), where the
  defect actually lives: a real edit driven through the shipped inspector, with the receipt open.
  That test is red today and green after the fix.
- **`rerender(` still lands in `SeedReceipt.test.tsx`** — Task 3 — but asserting the properties the
  component genuinely owns: that it is a pure projection of the snapshot it is handed, and that a
  NEW snapshot replaces the old one. Those are the fences that make the caller's snapshot both
  sufficient and non-permanent, and the second one is what forbids the wrong fix above.

Re-verify this reasoning against the live source in Task 1's `read_first` before accepting it. Four
of four executors in Phase 186 found an inherited claim false; this note is a claim like any other.
