# 240-04 — The screen says "conversation", and a disabled connection stops being offered

**Goal (SC#3, and D-240-19):** Opening a mail document shows the other messages of its
conversation, in order, from the shipped detail-panel shell — so `thread_key` is **read by
something a person can see**. And the Library's "From a connected source" picker stops offering
connections the operator has switched **off**.

## Why these two things are one plan

Both live on surfaces this phase is already changing, and both are gated by the same missing
thing: **`ConnectedSourceSection` and `CreateWatchModal` have no test suite at all** — the hole
`BUG-260908-02` came through, and the same hole a mail node would fall through. Building the
suite once serves both. Under G-8 this is one wave-sized unit, not two plans.

## ⚠ Load the design skill before touching the panel

`Skill("sketch-findings-agentic-rag")` — CLAUDE.md requires it for the document detail panel and
`ConfidenceChip`/inline-metadata surfaces. The Conversation section reuses the **Phase 117
relationships accordion shell**; it is not a new visual language, which is why G-2 does not fire
here (ROADMAP marks 240 *"UI hint (minimal)"* and, unlike 233/234/235, does not mandate a sketch).

<threat_model>
<trust_boundaries>
Browser (user) -> GET /documents/{id}/conversation -> RLS-scoped documents read
</trust_boundaries>
<threats>
- threat_id: TM-240-13
  category: Cross-user disclosure via an attacker-chosen grouping key
  component: GET /documents/{id}/conversation
  disposition: mitigated
  mitigation_plan: `thread_key` comes from a Message-ID a stranger wrote, so two unrelated users CAN be made to share one. The endpoint resolves the OPEN document first, under the caller's own auth, and then queries siblings scoped to that document's `user_id` — it NEVER accepts a `thread_key` from the client. A test asserts a caller cannot reach another user's message by planting a matching header.
- threat_id: TM-240-14
  category: Unbounded response / DoS
  component: GET /documents/{id}/conversation
  disposition: mitigated
  mitigation_plan: A mailing-list archive can share one key across thousands of messages. The endpoint caps at 200 siblings ordered by date and reports a truncation flag the UI renders honestly — never a silent slice. ⚠ PostgREST caps at 1000 by default, so an uncapped read would truncate INVISIBLY, which is the trap `_fetch_readability` already records in its own comments.
- threat_id: TM-240-15
  category: Ingest through a connection the operator switched off (BUG-260908-02)
  component: ConnectedSourceSection / CreateWatchModal
  disposition: mitigated
  mitigation_plan: The picker filters on `is_enabled`. ⚠ This is the SURFACE half only — the backend already refuses at `SourceRegistry.get_adapter` (`SourceConnectionDisabled`, Phase 239). Both halves stay; a UI filter is not a security control and the plan does not present it as one.
</threats>
</threat_model>
