<objective>
Stop the publish spine from painting a refusal as a full pass.

Purpose: D-186-11 warned that a new `blocked_stage` "falls through to a generic". Research
traced it and it is worse. `GauntletSpine` computes `blockedIndex = STAGES.findIndex(...)`,
which is `-1` for an unrecognised stage, and `PublishGauntlet.tsx:338` then reads
`isPassed = blockedIndex === -1 ? !running : i < blockedIndex` — so an unrecognised block
takes the **same branch as no block at all** and paints all eight stages green with ✓ badges,
while the headline prints `Blocked early — draft_changed` to a business user. A green
indicator scoped to the wrong thing is precisely the T-185-04-01 pattern, and it becomes
reachable the moment 186-02 ships.

`connReached` at `:342` carries the identical `-1` shape and is easy to miss — fixing only
`isPassed` leaves the connectors lighting up under an unknown block.

Output: a 9th `Commit` stage, both `-1` reads made fail-CLOSED, a code-keyed `BLOCKED_SENTENCE`
map in `verdictModel.ts`, and a test that renders a deliberately bogus stage and asserts zero
✓ badges — which protects **every future `blocked_stage`**, not just this one.

**Fix the property, not the patch.** That is the 185 lesson, verbatim.
</objective>
