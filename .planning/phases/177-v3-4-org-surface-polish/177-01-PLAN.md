COMPONENT (D-08), one RoleBadge / OrgIdentity element (D-04), and one HonestNotice (D-11) — so the

domain-specific. `statusChipMeta` is the invitation+SSO **lifecycle** mapper (the two surfaces whose
duplicated maps 177-03 consolidates). The members roster's **adoption** state is a DISTINCT domain and
keeps its own mapper in 177-04 (an active member reads calm/muted, not a wall of green success chips) —
so this plan does NOT claim `statusChipMeta` replaces every inline mapper.

scavenger hunt). Retiring the duplicated `CHIP_TONE_CLASS` maps, the 4-site role badge, and the
ad-hoc per-state callouts into single sources is the whole family-cohesion thesis.
Output: three presentational components + co-located vitest tests, exporting the tone/role/severity
mappings the consumers import.
