placed the thinking above it.

run finishes elsewhere, operator navigates back **without reloading**. A fence that only drives a
live send closes a criterion it cannot see.

answer on the three collateral surfaces the ROADMAP names.

⛔ **No streaming-architecture rewrite** (D-243-08). If the navigation-path defect turns out to live
in the reconcile rather than in the branch, the fix is still surgical — or it is a stated finding
and a seed, not a restructure.
