<objective>
Build the catalog: a read surface over rows that already exist, listing **every Expert the caller
may actually use and none they may not**.

sketch puts it plainly: *"a catalog advertising locked Experts is a brochure for a locked door."*

owns no filtering opinion of its own. D-262-02 forbids the grey-out and forbids the upsell; the UAT
row is **"the card VANISHES"**, never "the card is disabled".

Output: the catalog page, its card, and the pure module both are built on.
</objective>
