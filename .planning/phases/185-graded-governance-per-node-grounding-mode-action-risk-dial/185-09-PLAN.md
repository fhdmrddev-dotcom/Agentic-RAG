border) plus a sealed-edge border reinforcement, driven by the `grounded` boolean plan 185-08 threaded
onto the node data.

Purpose: SPEC Req 6, sketch 143-A. The constraint that made this hard is the reason the mark is shape:
137-B banks ALL colour for Phase 188's run status, and both card word-badge slots are committed to
188/189. So governance may spend neither colour nor a badge.

border. The reading degrades from two carriers to one; it never disappears. Two non-negotiables follow:
the seal may NEVER be conditional on run state, and top-right of the card is permanently CLAIMED —
Phases 188/189 may not take it.
