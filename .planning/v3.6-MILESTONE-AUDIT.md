### The one warning, stated precisely

`POST /workflows/generate` never branches on `bundle.degraded`. During a folder/skill registry
outage, `/validate` and `/workflows/grounding-bundle` both degrade **honestly** — but the NL seed
silently produces a folder-blind draft presented as `{"ok": true}`. VOCAB-02's
"safe-by-construction" claim holds on the happy path and not during an outage.

Capped at WARNING rather than BLOCKER because the draft still passes `/validate` and the publish
gauntlet before anything can ship. Nothing unsafe publishes; the author gets a misleadingly
confident first draft.

Tracked as **SEED-133**, whose re-open trigger read *"when Phase 187 is scoped"* — 187 shipped
without closing it. That trigger has already fired and should be re-armed against Phase 189 or a
cleanup phase.
