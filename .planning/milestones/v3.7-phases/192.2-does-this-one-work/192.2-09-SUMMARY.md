**A filtered or searched row now says which property put it there — `Matched: Strict`, `Matched: Makes a file`, `Matched: Your words in its purpose` — while the RESTING card renders no such node at all, proven by the D-03 characterization pin passing byte-unchanged and unedited.**

## Accomplishments

### Task 1 — `matchReasons`, the derivation (`7b62057a`)

`libraryFilter.ts` gains a closed union `MatchReason = "makes-a-file" | "strict" | "purpose"` and
`matchReasons(row, selection)`. Three rules, and each is guarded by its own case:

1. `"makes-a-file"` iff the chip is pressed **and** `CHIP_PREDICATES["makes-a-file"](row)`.
2. `"strict"` iff the chip is pressed **and** `CHIP_PREDICATES.strict(row)`.
3. `"purpose"` iff the trimmed needle is non-empty, the NAME does **not** carry it, and
   `business_requirement` does.

The four remaining chips emit nothing — they already have a visible carrier on line 2 or in the
identity line, and echoing them is the noise D-03 removed.

**The "do not write a second normalization" instruction was taken literally rather than
approximately.** `matchesQuery` and `matchReasons` now share one `needleOf` / `carries` pair,
extracted in this commit; `matchesQuery`'s behaviour is unchanged (its 6 shipped search cases pass
untouched) but there is no longer a second `.trim().toLowerCase()` anywhere in the module.

`libraryVocabulary.ts` gains `MATCH_REASON_PREFIX`, `MATCH_REASON_PURPOSE`, `MATCH_REASON_WORDS`
and `MATCH_REASON_SEPARATOR`. Two of the three words are **read** from `CHIP_WORDS`, so the chip a
person pressed and the reason the row answers with are one value, not two literals that agree today.

`libraryFilter.test.ts`: **64 → 84** cases.

### Task 2 — the card renders it, and cannot at rest (`033a4bc3`)

`WorkflowCardProps.matchReasons?: readonly MatchReason[]`, defaulting to one module-scoped frozen
empty array. On an empty array the component renders **no node at all** — not an empty node, not a
zero-height wrapper. When non-empty it renders one `data-testid="row-match-reason"` div as the
**last child** of the `min-w-0` column, below the identity line and the folder chip, at the identity
line's own 11px muted scale (`MATCH_REASON_CLASSES` **reads** `IDENTITY_CLASSES` rather than
restating it, so no third secondary size exists).

Placement is asserted by **child order**, never by class name — 5 children with a folder chip, 4
without, and each index bound to the node its testid resolves to.

`WorkflowCard.test.tsx`: **105 → 122** cases.

### Task 3 — the page resolves it (`6139bb7d`)

The selection object, previously assembled inline inside the `filterLibrary` memo, is hoisted into
its own `useMemo` and handed to **both** consumers. `grep -n "chips: activeChips"` returns exactly
one line (473), where it previously returned exactly one inline occurrence at 460:

```
473:    () => ({ query, chips: activeChips, projectId: selectedProjectId }),
```

`matchReasons={matchReasons(row, selection)}` at the card call site — the third page-resolved prop
of exactly the kind `hasExistingFork` and `identity` already are, with a comment in the file's
register saying why only the page can answer it.

`WorkflowsPage.test.tsx`: **55 → 59** cases (no decrease — it is a pinned file).

### 2. [Plan latitude] `MATCH_REASON_PURPOSE` ships as `Your words in its purpose`

- **Found during:** Task 1, while composing the line.
- **Issue:** The plan's **suggested** literals were `Matched:` and `Matched in its purpose`. Composed,
  they render `Matched: Matched in its purpose` — a stutter — and the purpose word would have been
  the only reason phrased as a verb while the two chip reasons are noun phrases read out of
  `CHIP_WORDS`.
- **Fix:** The prefix ships as suggested (`Matched:`); the purpose word ships as
  `Your words in its purpose`, so the line reads `Matched: Makes a file · Your words in its purpose`.
  The plan's own tense requirement is met by the composed line — the lead-in carries the past tense
  for all three reasons rather than each word carrying it separately. Both the original suggestion
  and this reasoning are recorded at the export's docblock, not just here.
- **Files modified:** `frontend/src/components/workflows/library/libraryVocabulary.ts`
- **Commit:** `7b62057a`

### 3. [Plan latitude] `needleOf` / `carries` extracted rather than the needle re-normalized

- The plan said *"do not write a second normalization"*. The only way to honour that literally was to
  give `matchesQuery` and `matchReasons` one shared pair, which is a (tiny) edit to a shipped
  function's body. Its behaviour is provably unchanged — the module's six shipped search cases pass
