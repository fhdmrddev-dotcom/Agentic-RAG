<decisions>
**DEC-09-A — THE REPAIR IS (a) A MINIMAL ON-CARD SIGNAL, SCOPED TO THE FILTERED STATE ONLY — and
here is why that does not re-litigate D-03.**

The review offered (a) surface the selecting fact when the chip is active, mirroring the shipped
`HighlightTitle` pattern for the search, or (b) retire the two chips in the same commit that removed
their carriers. **(a) is chosen, and narrowed further: the line is structurally unable to render on a
resting card**, because the prop that feeds it is empty whenever no chip is pressed and no search is
typed.

- **D-03 governs the RESTING card.** Its own words: *"Six atoms leave the **resting** card."* A card
  explaining why it matched a filter the user just applied is not the resting card, and the
  characterization pin proves the distinction mechanically rather than rhetorically: the pin renders
  with no selection, so it must come through this plan **byte-unchanged**.
- **The precedent is already shipped on this exact surface.** `HighlightTitle` marks *why this row
  matched* on the NAME. This is the same idea for the two facts the name cannot carry.
- **(b) was rejected as out-of-envelope.** Retiring two working filters removes user-facing
  capability inside a gap-closure round, on a product decision nobody made. G-7 caps what a round may
  ADD; deleting shipped capability by planner discretion is worse, not safer.

⚠ **THE SUBTRACTION IS STILL THE FEATURE, and this plan must not become a back door for the six cut
atoms.** Only THREE reasons are ever emitted, and the other three chips emit nothing — `ready-to-run`,
`yours`, `still-building` and `starters` all already have a visible carrier on line 2 or the mark, and
echoing them would be noise the design deliberately withholds. Adding a fourth reason later is a
decision, not a tidy-up.

**DEC-09-B — `matchReasons` lives in `libraryFilter.ts`, not in a new module.** The reason a row
matched belongs to the module that owns the matching; a separate module would be a second home for
one question, which is the exact drift this subtree's fences exist to prevent. It also avoids a new
entry in `LIBRARY_SUBTREE_PATHS` — though note that if a new module HAD been created, adding it there
in the same commit would be mandatory, because **an unlisted module is swept by six fences NOT AT ALL
while all six stay green.**

**DEC-09-C — the reason DERIVES, it never re-implements.** `matchReasons` calls
`CHIP_PREDICATES["makes-a-file"]` and `CHIP_PREDICATES.strict` rather than re-deriving
`soulDeliverable` / `tierForDefinition`. A second copy of a predicate is a second answer to one
question, which is `libraryFilter.ts`'s own stated rule (*"derive, do not re-implement"*).

**DEC-09-D — the words are IMPORTED, never re-spelled.** The two chip reasons read
`CHIP_WORDS["makes-a-file"].label` and `CHIP_WORDS.strict.label`, so the chip and the reason cannot
drift into two spellings of one word. Only the purpose reason and the line's lead-in are new strings,
and both live in `libraryVocabulary.ts` — nowhere else.

**G-5.** `WorkflowCard.tsx` fires (`12/4/1105`) and its obligation is **DISCHARGED** (Wave 2 of this
phase), with the next seam NAMED: the `⋯` overflow menu and its armed-delete state machine. **This
plan does not touch that seam** — it adds one leaf line in the `min-w-0` column — so the discharge
holds and no new obligation arises. `WorkflowsPage.tsx` (`36/14/1186`) reads *satisfied (192/192.1)*;
this plan adds one resolved prop at the existing call site, the third of its kind, honoured by
construction. `libraryFilter.ts` (`4/4/341`) and `libraryVocabulary.ts` (`8/4/584`) both fire and both
gain their OWN concern, not a second one. **Re-derive all four triples and record them** — five rows
were found stale on 2026-08-17 and three read `satisfied`.
</decisions>
