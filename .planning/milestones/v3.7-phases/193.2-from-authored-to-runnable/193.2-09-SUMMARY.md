| no trim / empty-check / null-coercion | ✅ `grep` of the changed lines for `.trim()` → **0** |
| the key strategy is chosen and commented with its reason | ✅ see below |

**WHY `false` RATHER THAN `delete`, and the two are not interchangeable here.** `definitionOps.ts`
deletes because `name` / `name_seeded_by_ai` are **optional** keys where absence is the only honest
spelling of "no name", and an explicit `undefined` would serialize into a backend union with
`extra="forbid"`. This field differs in the one way that matters: `WorkflowDefinition` **DECLARES**
it, `bool = False`, with no `| None` (plan 193.2-07), so `false` is a declared valid value that
`extra="forbid"` accepts and that means exactly what an absent key means — **there is no round-trip
identity to protect.** What `false` buys is **EVIDENCE**: the demotion becomes directly observable on
the PATCH body, whereas an absent key is indistinguishable from a store that never wrote the field at
all. That claim is not an argument — **plant 4 below reds the exact assertion when the `delete`
strategy is substituted.** `undefined` is refused outright; that is the trap `definitionOps` avoided.

⚠ **The honest whitespace consequence, in the comment rather than smoothed away:** editing a seeded
value down to `"   "` clears the flag AND leaves a whitespace value. Correct on both halves — the
server's stamp would refuse to mark such a value either, and `REQUIREMENT_INVITATION` does not show
for a whitespace value. Today's behaviour, unchanged.
