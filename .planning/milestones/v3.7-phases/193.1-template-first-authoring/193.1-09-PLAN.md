<objective>
Close SC#3: when a template is attached to an already-drafted workflow, say which of its fields the
draft names — honestly, without ever claiming a coverage verdict the app cannot compute.

Purpose: the shipped draft-then-attach path (Phase 193 + quick task `260814-q5r`) binds a template to
a draft that was written without one, and says nothing about the fit. **Nothing in the app can
compute a coverage verdict at attach time** — `check_coverage`
(`template_render_service.py:416-460`) runs at RUN time over an emitted field map with values; at
attach time there are only names. `reconcile.disclaim` is therefore the load-bearing string on the
surface, and D-10 is a red line: three buckets, never two.

Output: one pure classifier, one component, one gated line.
</objective>
