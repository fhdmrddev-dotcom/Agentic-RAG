<objective>
Turn a nameless quiet button into a named, expected input — and stop showing it to the ~34 published
workflows that positively cannot use it.

Purpose: SC#3's other half. Today the upload renders on **every** workflow, unlabelled, so most rows
carry a control they cannot use — the discoverability problem inverted. Nothing is lost by hiding it
on a positive no: handing a template to a workflow with no fill phase never did anything.

⚠ **D-20's asymmetry with D-15 is DELIBERATE and must not be "fixed".** On the card, silence costs
nothing. In this modal, hiding on `unknown` **removes a shipped capability (WFIN-01)** from a user who
may genuinely need it and has no way to discover it existed — and `unknown` is **110 of 145 published
rows**. A backend hiccup, or a frontend deployed ahead of its backend, must not silently strip the
feature.

⚠ **`launchError` is NOT template-only.** `handleRun`'s catch sets it on **any** `onRun` rejection and
its node lives INSIDE the wrapper D-17 removes. Hiding the block naively makes launch failures silent
on every non-template workflow — the WR-03 class of defect Phase 192's gap round already had to repair
on this same surface.

Output: the label, the render condition that cuts around the error node, three-arm coverage, and one
recorded re-capture of the six DOM baselines.
</objective>
