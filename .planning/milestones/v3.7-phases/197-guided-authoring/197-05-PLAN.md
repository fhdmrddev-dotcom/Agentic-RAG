## Two decisions taken here rather than discovered

1. **The action writes `name` and NOTHING else. It does NOT stamp a provenance flag.** C-1 measured
   that `name_seeded_by_ai` is a **`PhaseSpec`** field (`harness.py:435`) — a *step*-name flag.
   `WorkflowDefinition` has no equivalent (15 fields, none of them it), so *"the row wears the AI
   mark"* is a net-new additive-optional field plus a server-side stamp plus a demote-on-edit — the
   whole of `193.2-07`'s pattern, a wave rather than a checkbox. **Declined for this phase with the
   reason recorded** (the row's own sentence already says the AI chose the name; D-16 forbids the
   mark meaning anything more). **Re-open trigger:** any phase that adds a definition-level
   provenance field for another reason — row 4's mark comes along free at that point.
   ⚠ **Consequence for this plan:** `setName`'s `set()` writes exactly one `meta` key. It takes
   `setProjectFolder`'s simpler shape, not `setBusinessRequirement`'s two-key one.
2. **`BuilderDefinition` is NOT widened with `name?: string`.** `meta.name` lands under the index
   signature and types as `unknown`; the ONE read site (the page, which passes the value down to the
   card) narrows with `typeof meta.name === "string"` — the shipped idiom two lines from where the
   page already works (`WorkflowBuilderPage.tsx:2158` does exactly this for the requirement). This
   keeps a G-5-firing 2398-line page's type surface untouched for the cost of one expression.
   ⚠ Writing is unaffected either way: `{...s.meta, name}` assigns a `string` under an
   `unknown`-typed index key and typechecks.
