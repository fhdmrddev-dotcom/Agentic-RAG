<objective>
Build the parent that makes the arrival moment **one card**.

Purpose: sketch 174, operator-corrected twice, is the acceptance bar. Measured on a 780 px screen:
one card is **149 px** of chrome leaving the workflow **507 px (65%)**; two cards are 284/363 (47%);
two cards opened are 478/169 (**22%**). **Merging halves the arrival chrome.** And it is not only a
design preference — `graphColumn` is a three-row grid with `[&>*:last-child]:row-start-3`, so a
**fourth child strands the graph at 0 px**. One card is what keeps the layout valid.

**D-04 — the surface arrives with the draft and is DISMISSIBLE**, and that is what keeps it an offer
rather than a wall. `SeedReceipt` already sets the precedent of a card that arrives unasked, and its
`open` prop is already caller-owned, so the parent inherits the contract rather than inventing one.
**D-01 — the guidance is ON THE DRAFT, AFTER**: this card exists only once a draft has landed, so
nothing is added to the generation's critical path.

⚠ **This is a COMPOSITION change, not a charter change, and the plan must not blur them.** Nothing
widens `SeedReceipt`. It stays the leaf it is — *"authors no sentence of its own"*, *"declares no
predicate of its own"*, *"imports nothing from the API client"* — and a PARENT composes its output
with the decisions list. **One card in the UI, two components underneath.** D-02 survives intact, and
`SeedReceipt.tsx` is under a `0 0` numstat criterion from plan `197-01`.

Output: `DraftArrivalCard.tsx` and its suite. Mounted by nobody yet — plan `197-09` mounts it.

## The composition detail, DECIDED here rather than discovered by an executor

`SeedReceipt` renders its own card frame, its own heading, its own ✕ and its own closing line
(`SeedReceipt.tsx:239-357`). The one-card shape needs those four things ONCE, at the card level,
with the receipt's grounding paragraphs and sealed rows behind a fold. Since the receipt may not be
edited, the parent:

- **owns the frame, the heading, the ✕ and the closing line**, rendering the receipt's OWN shipped
  constants and formatter from `definitionOps` (`seedReceiptHeading`, `SEED_RECEIPT_DISMISS_LABEL`,
  `SEED_RECEIPT_DISMISS_GLYPH`, `SEED_RECEIPT_NOTHING_COMMITTED`) — composed, never re-typed;
- **renders `<SeedReceipt open={groundingOpen} …/>` inside the first fold's body**, wrapped in a div
  whose arbitrary child variants neutralise the receipt's own frame and suppress its now-duplicate
  heading, ✕ and closing line, each targeted by the receipt's shipped `data-testid` handles.

⚠ **Two consequences stated rather than left implied.** (1) `display:none` removes the suppressed ✕
from the tab order, so there is no hidden focusable control — that is why suppression is CSS rather
than opacity. (2) The receipt's `<section>` loses its accessible name when its `<h2>` is suppressed;
it is a generic unnamed region inside a named parent section, which is benign because an unnamed
`<section>` is not exposed as a landmark. Both are accepted deliberately.

⚠ **jsdom applies no CSS, so the suppression is proved by a SOURCE assertion plus G-4 row U1.**
This project's own hardest-won lesson governs here: *geometry proves composition; only looking proves
appearance.* Three pages of green assertions did not notice that every sketch page rendered in LIGHT
mode, nor that the header contradicted the card. **U1 is the appearance proof and it is owed.**
