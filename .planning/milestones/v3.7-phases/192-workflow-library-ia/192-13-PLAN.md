<objective>
Give the library the WORDS for the two facts the shipped surface cannot currently say: *you already
have a copy of this* and *your click failed*.

Purpose: the U5 blocker is not only that the fork 409s in silence — it is that the card's one
consequence sentence will be a QUIET LIE the moment plan `192-14` changes what the verb does. D-13's
sentence promises "Opens a new private copy you can edit." Under the operator's 2026-08-11 decision,
a row you already forked opens your EXISTING draft instead. A user who states that consequence before
clicking (which is verbatim SC#3) would then be wrong. Trading a silent failure for a quiet lie is not
closing this gap.

Output: two new vocabulary exports, one new optional `WorkflowCard` prop that defaults to the shipped
behaviour byte-for-byte, and the tests that pin both variants.
</objective>
