<objective>
Close the U5 blocker's first half: the fork verb must stop being a permanently dead button on any
published row whose slug already carries the version it tries to mint.

Purpose: `192-UAT.md` test 11. An operator clicked `⋯ → Make my own copy` on *Compliance Gap Report*,
got **HTTP 409 twice with zero user-visible signal**, and `workflow_definitions` stayed at 222. The
cause is measured, not suspected: `onTweak` reads `def.version` off the JSONB `definition`, where it
is NULL on every row (the real version is the `version` COLUMN), so `nextVersion` is hard-wired to 2
in practice, and `UNIQUE(slug, version)` is GLOBAL. **Re-measured at plan time: 18 slugs in the live
DB carry more than one version, and 16 of those 18 are exactly `v1 published + v2 draft`.**

The operator's locked decision (2026-08-11) is to OPEN THE EXISTING DRAFT rather than mint the next
free version — chosen because it removes the failure class for those 16 rather than making the
collision rarer, and because it needs no backend change and no wire-type change.

Output: one lookup, one early branch, one prop, and the regression test that enters the branch the
whole shipped suite could not reach.
</objective>
