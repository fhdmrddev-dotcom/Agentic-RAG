## Deviations from Plan

### 1. [Rule 3 — blocking] `useEffect` reset-on-open is forbidden by the scoped lint

The plan says to copy `InviteMemberDialog.tsx:85-100`'s reset-on-open **effect**. Doing so failed
`npx eslint src/components/workflows/library/` outright with `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect`
