### 1. [Rule 1 — Measurement] The plan's *"no `title` attribute anywhere in the rendered card"* is FALSE at HEAD, and was measured false BEFORE one line of this phase existed

- **Found during:** Task 1's first RED run.
- **Issue:** the plan's behavior list asserts it flatly. Driven against the **UNEDITED** card:


  **All three hits belong to the shared soul this card consumes unchanged** —
  `WorkflowSoul.tsx:99` (`title={tier.description}` on the tier chip) and `PhaseSpine.tsx:77`
  (`title={name || undefined}`, once per phase glyph; the fixture definition carries two phases,
  so 1 + 2 = 3).
- **Why it is not a defect:** `CLAUDE.md`'s Phase-192 ledger row already records **both by name**
  as INHERITED and out of scope by D-01, and **F1 — the fence that actually binds — sweeps SOURCE
  modules under `library/`**, where the count is genuinely zero. Measured on this file:
  `grep -n "title=" WorkflowCard.tsx` → **nothing**, before and after.
- **Fix:** the rendered assertion is scoped to the card's OWN chrome, with the soul excluded **by
  its shipped testid** rather than by a class guess — and a **NON-VACUITY control** asserts the
  carve-out really is drawn around three inherited nodes (`card` 3, `soul` 3) rather than around
  zero, so the exclusion can never quietly become the loophole that hides a real one. The
  positive control stays.
- ⚠ **The distinction that makes this worth a paragraph is D-36's, one level down:** F1's scoping
  control covers an object *property*, not a component *prop*, which is how the D-07 highlight got
  deferred. Here the same gap runs the other way — a **rendered-DOM** sweep is strictly wider than
  the **source** fence, and widening a check without widening its scope produces a failure that
  looks like the plan's fault.
