- **Run ID:** `bef870ba-74f4-41d5-aa78-3fa407421396`
- **Centre Column Content:** Top `RunHero` card with deliverable answer markdown and download file button (`renewal-letter.docx (18.4 KB)`), `RunReceipt` summary strip with total runtime, followed by `RunStepList` step cards displaying step titles with declared counts (`15 sources`, `2 fields`) and `wrote an answer`.

Steps with no citations degrade to non-expandable cards without chevrons.

### Row 7: Long Answer Scrolling (Long-Message Axis)
