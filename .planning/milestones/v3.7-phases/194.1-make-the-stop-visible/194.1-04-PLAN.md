<objective>
Build `<StopControl threadId variant>` — the ONE component every Stop mount renders — and land the
composer mount on it.

Purpose: Phase 194 **D-08** binds the runtime half (*four mounts, ONE mechanism*). This is the display
half of that same rule: four mounts that acknowledge a press four different ways satisfies D-08 and
leaves the user exactly as confused. Sketch **168-B** settles the shape — on press the control
**leaves its slot** and `⊘ Stopping this run…` takes it. **No disabled button, because no button**: a
double-press is impossible by construction rather than defended.

Output: one component with three render arms (resting control / stopping reading / not-confirmed
reading beside a pressable control), reading two primitive selectors from plan 03 and owning **zero**
state; plus the composer wired to it.

⚠ **D-22 correction, and it changes what "wire the composer" means.** The composer does **not** go
through `stopThread` today — `ChatArea.tsx:361 onStop={stopStreaming}` → `useMessages.ts:118
stopStreaming: actions.stopStream`. Routing it at `stopThread(threadId)` per D-05 is
behaviour-preserving (`stopStream` resolves `activeThreadIdRef.current`, which **is** the composer's
thread, and after plan 03 the two bodies are mirrors) and it retires the second resolver as a
consequence rather than as extra scope.
</objective>
