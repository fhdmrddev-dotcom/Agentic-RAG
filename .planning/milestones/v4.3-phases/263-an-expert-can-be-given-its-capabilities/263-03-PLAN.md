⛔ **"This phase’s base commit" is DEFINED in `263-01-PLAN.md`’s objective and its SHA is recorded

re-derive it: after wave 1 lands, a re-derived base would compare HEAD against itself and the
inventory check would pass vacuously.
Output: two edits to `api/experts.py`, one typed error carrier and one new client function, and the
D-263-11 measurement with a fresh non-vacuity plant.

⛔ **THE 422 BECOMES A 400 IF IT LANDS IN THE WRONG PLACE — measured.** `create_expert`
(`api/experts.py:73-85`) wraps its service call in a bare `except Exception`, and `HTTPException`
subclasses `Exception`. A 422 raised inside that `try` is caught at `:80`, logged as an error, and
re-raised as a **400 whose detail is the stringified exception** — D-263-10's named refusal
destroyed, the client silently falling back to re-deriving. This is precisely this project's
recurring "a green fence coexisting with the shipped defect" shape.
</objective>
