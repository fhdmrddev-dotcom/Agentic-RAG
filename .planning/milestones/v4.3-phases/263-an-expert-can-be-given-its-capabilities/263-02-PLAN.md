Make the drafter name what it cannot find, and make the body it later writes provably borrowed
rather than invented.

Purpose: PACK-14's backend half (the draft returns the skills that do NOT exist, as a required
field) and PACK-15's reuse claim (the body-authoring module is a DRIVER over `skill-creator`'s own
craft doctrine, not a second authoring engine).

module, and the fence that makes D-263-13 falsifiable rather than rhetorical.

⛔ **D-263-04's LITERAL WORDING IS REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT AND MUST NOT BE BUILT AS WRITTEN.**
`skill-creator` is a DB row whose `instructions` are a SEVEN-STEP INTERACTIVE HUMAN INTERVIEW LOOP
("Move one step at a time and keep the user in control"), naming five tools a sealed shot cannot
expose, whose step 3 tells the model to call `save_skill` — which conflicts with the forced emitter
AND with D-263-03. There is no callable `skill-creator` anywhere in `backend/app/` or
`frontend/src/`. D-263-13 is the operator-ratified correction: reuse it as DATA (its §3 craft block,
read from the DB at call time), never as an engine.
