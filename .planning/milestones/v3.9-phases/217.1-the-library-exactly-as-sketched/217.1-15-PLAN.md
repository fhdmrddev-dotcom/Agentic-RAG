path, transition the attribute) — a pure leaf: `{ weeks: { week: string; count: number }[] }` renders a
polyline. It receives its data as a PROP computed from the `Found by` section's already-fetched `/queries`
rows (bucketed by `asked_at`), not a second fetch.
