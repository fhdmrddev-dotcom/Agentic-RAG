## Decisions Made

Beyond the frontmatter list, two are worth stating in full because a later wave could read them the other way:

**The slice is balanced, and K7 is the fence for the bug that proves why.** The obvious walker takes each `data-block` marker to the next one. On `v-h` the REJECTED arm-1 tile (`Retrieval Score`, D-217.1-03) sits *after* the checks table in document order, so a lazy slice records it as one of `checks`'s atoms — quietly wrong, still green, the same family as the stripper bug the file's own docblock records. `elementSlice()` counts tag depth instead, and **K7 asserts `Retrieval Score` leaks into no block's atoms** while **K7b asserts the qualified arm IS carried as its own block**. Both are green.

**The `match-strength-tile` position is the sketch's, and that is not a contradiction of D-217.1-29.** The sketch draws it last, inside the side-by-side arm that asked the operator to choose; D-217.1-29 rules it into the Health tile row as the fifth tile. The contract records the sketch's own order **because the contract is generated** — but the fence asserts **presence, not position**, so the ruled placement stays the build's to honour. Both the JSON's ordering and this reasoning are stated in the emitted markdown, so a reader of the contract alone cannot mistake one for the other.

- **Found during:** Task 1
