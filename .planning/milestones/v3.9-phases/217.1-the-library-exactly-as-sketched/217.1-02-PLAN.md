label. Add a `short` field to `termMap.ts`'s six `ingest.*` `Term` entries (interface at `:26-33`, additive

failure for the G-4 `Needs attention` row). The default arm for an unrecognised message MUST keep the
shipped honest fallback (`"It stopped, and no reason was recorded."`), never invent a cause.
