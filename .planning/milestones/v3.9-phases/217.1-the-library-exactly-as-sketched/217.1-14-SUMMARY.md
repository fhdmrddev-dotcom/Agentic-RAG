## Decisions

1. **`as never` instead of `satisfies never`.** Ternary chains in TypeScript don't narrow the else arm to `never` when all union members are covered. The exhaustiveness guarantee is provided by renameFence.test.ts's static assertion that ActiveView members match ChatLayout branches.

2. **KnowledgeHealthPage.tsx and GovernancePage.tsx deleted.** Confirmed no remaining imports from source files (only comments/docblocks reference them). The pages are fully superseded by the Library's Health tab (Plan 12).

3. **GovernancePage.test.tsx also deleted.** It tested the standalone Governance page surface which no longer exists — its role is fulfilled by the Library's Health tab signal chips.
