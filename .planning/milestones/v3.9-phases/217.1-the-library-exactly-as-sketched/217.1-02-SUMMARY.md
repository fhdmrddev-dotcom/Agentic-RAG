- **Duration:** ~45 min (resumed from a prior session that committed task 1)
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 10

- The six-segment ingestion strip now renders VISIBLE short labels (Read, Split, Index, Tables, Images, Label) instead of six blank sr-only boxes
- A failed ingest shows one plain-language sentence classified from the raw Postgres error dict; the raw string is reachable only behind the Technical-names reveal
