<objective>
Take the verb out of the two places it ORGANISES the node form — the picker's capability-scoped read
and the three-verb radio group — and put the connection's own tool list in its place. The verb stays
on the row as an attribute; it stops being an axis.

Purpose: D-211-12 requires the tool-list readers to stop gating on `mcp_server_url`, or plan 211-01's
descriptors ship where no surface can display them — this project's built-but-unreachable signature.
⚠ **And the naive form of that fix creates a WORSE unreachability, which is why this plan is worded
the way it is.** See Task 1's preamble; it is the load-bearing part of this plan.

Output: the minimum node-form change that makes SC#1/SC#3 true and D-211-12 real, with **no layout
change** (D-211-07), plus the render-gate guards that prove the loop cannot close again.
</objective>
