row. State that in the constant's docblock, and state that Phase 212 owns its content.

the field set the panel renders, read through `own(...)`, **total by construction**: an unknown
identity resolves to the new `"service"` member. Presentation only — it decides which credential
fields are shown, nothing else.

**(d) Extend `ConnectionShape` with a `"service"` member and give `FIELD_COUNTS` its bound count**,
per that constant's own rule ("a fifth member added without a bound count is a TYPE ERROR here").
The service shape renders the Name field and the Service field and nothing else — it is CONN-08's row.

**(e) Rename `capabilityLabelOf`** to a service-facing reader with the behaviour above. Keep every
call site compiling; name the rename in the summary.

**(f) `configFromDraft` and the validity arms keep their existing per-shape arms** — those are
attribute logic and CONN-05 requires them to keep working. Add the `"service"` arm: empty config, no
