<objective>
Hand the describe door the author's **connected services and granted tools** as its vocabulary, and
refuse — by name, with a next action, drafting nothing — when their prose names a service they have
not connected.

Purpose: STEP-06, closing `SEED-208`. SC#5's own words name the failure: *"an invented step that
validates and fails at 03:00."* ⚠ **Post-draft validation alone was rejected because it is a
model-behaviour guarantee** — it depends on the model, or a checker, noticing. The picker makes the
failure structurally impossible: the generator's vocabulary *is* the ticked set.

Output: a picker on the describe screen, a whole-word matcher with an honest fallback, the refusal,
and a backend constraint that is **enforced on the emitted definition** rather than merely prompted.
</objective>

| author prose → `DOOR_REFUSAL(service)` | ⚠ Untrusted free text is matched against a catalog and interpolated into a rendered sentence. |
