<objective>
Turn `WorkflowDefinition.inputs[]` from a hint line into real fields on the library Run modal and the
schedule modal, and carry their values to the run.

Purpose: SC#2 says *from EVERY launch path* and names the library, a thread and a schedule
explicitly — **a launcher that silently sends nothing fails the criterion.** The `InputFieldSpec`
machinery already exists (`harness.py:537-567`); `RunModal` deliberately renders it as a hint line
*"because nothing guaranteed the value would be used"*. **D-214-04 supplies the guarantee**, and this
plan is where that shipped refusal's condition stops holding.

Output: two launchers rendering real fields, one `onRun` contract widened, and the six whole-`innerHTML`
captures re-baselined deliberately rather than discovered.
</objective>
