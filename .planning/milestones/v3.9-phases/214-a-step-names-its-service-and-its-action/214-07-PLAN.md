`ArgumentRowModel` per DECLARED property — never one per `tool_args` key. `required` comes from the
schema's `required` array. `renderable` comes from the mirrored predicate: `string` / `number` /
`integer` / `boolean` / scalar `enum` are renderable; `object`, `array`, `oneOf`/`anyOf`/`allOf`,
`$ref` and an absent `type` are not.

is no "old steps keep the old editor" arm: one authoring surface, or SC#1 stays false for every

phase's slug>` and `preset: true`. ⚠ **`preset` is a DERIVED display flag, never stored** — the note
it drives (`ARG_PRESET_NOTE`) is the visible form of a rule that used to be invisible, and storing it
would make a derived fact go stale.

`leftovers` are `toolArgs` keys the schema does not declare. ⚠ They are **surfaced, never dropped** —
the adapter already fails closed on an undeclared key, so a silent drop would hide a step that was
