## What I checked and found sound

- **EXT-01 text.** `docs/EXTENSION-CONTRACT.md` states the core law word for word. It names the three permitted mechanisms and all three refusals SEED-291 asks for (third-party executors/emitters/validators, a generic HTTP egress node, branching/looping graphs as a plugin concern). CLAUDE.md carries the cross-reference.
- **The registries match the tests at HEAD.** 7 executors, 2 programmatic functions (`split_topic`, `eval_slow_step`), 1 emitter (`render_template`), 29 tools, 10 validator kinds, and an empty `PROGRAMMATIC_VALIDATOR_REGISTRY`. `GUARD` exit=0 and `PYTEST` 6 passed on the clean tree.
- **Refusal 2 holds in code.** `EXTERNAL_ACTION_CAPABILITIES` is a closed frozenset (`send_email`, `create_ticket`, `post_message`). `descriptors.py:153` refuses anything outside it. No webhook/`http_request` capability exists. `validate_mcp_destination` enforces https plus public addresses (driven, R-07).
- **The `importlib` sites outside the six paths are all closed-dict lookups, not data-driven resolution:** `connectors/registry.py:72` (`_ADAPTERS[capability]`, with its key set asserted equal to the closed capability set), `provider_gateway/dispatcher.py:125` (`_SURFACE_ADAPTERS.get`, which raises on an unknown surface) and `connectors/service_tools.py:1952` (`_GOOGLE_READ_CALLS` lookup). They are consistent with the law's intent. They also show that "no importlib" is a proxy for the law, not the law itself.
- **The settings.json claim `34 → 36` is accurate.** The two new lines are `"command"` and `"type": "command"`. Nothing was dropped (diff of the grep sets).
- **`npm run check:extension-contract` works from `frontend/`** (exit 0). The plan named a root `package.json`, which does not exist, so the builder wired it into `frontend/package.json`. This is a benign deviation.
- The six trigger files contain no `eval(` / `exec(` / `importlib` / `__import__` outside comments.
