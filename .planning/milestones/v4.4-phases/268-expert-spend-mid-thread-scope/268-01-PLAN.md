born, and stop a Deep Continue from erasing the paused segment's tokens (METER-08's data half).

active org on every row a turn writes (D-268-07 / D-268-22, SEED-314), makes Continue accumulate
(D-268-20), and proves the org stamps against real Postgres for the two-org user the trigger gets wrong.

kwargs, the send-path resolve-once, four `agent_loop` org stamps, continuation accumulation, a call-site
disposition fence, a real-PG two-org integration test, `268-BASELINES.md`, and the `db/runs.py` ledger
section.
