### The two gaps, and why they were real

Both were decisions the phase **recorded in prose and never asserted** — the failure mode where
a truth is written into a `must_haves` block, believed thereafter, and guarded by nothing.

**GAP-1 — D-184.1-03 (the Spine view).** The decision reads *"with the flag ON the merged header
also appears in the Spine view … recorded as a decision rather than discovered."* No assertion
existed. The regression it now catches: the merged row becoming **tab-conditional** — drawn on
Canvas, silently dropped back toward three bands on Spine.

> **An honest finding from writing it.** Spine is *already the default landing view*
> (`graphView` cold-starts at `"spine"`, D-183-02), so every earlier flag-on case was exercising
> Spine **incidentally**. The new case drives the tab **explicitly** through the real control and
> asserts `aria-selected` on both tabs, so it keeps its meaning if that default ever flips. This
> is disclosed inline in the test rather than left to be rediscovered.

**GAP-2 — D-184.1-04's non-breach half.** The decision's load-bearing claim is that *"the fetching
`useEffectiveFeatures()` has ZERO non-test call sites — the single FETCH in `App.tsx` is
unchanged."* That claim is what makes three gate call sites legal under D-183-03, and it was true
on measurement — but unguarded. A future component adding its own fetch would have shipped green.
The guard strips comments before counting (this file's own `stripComments`, so a docblock naming
the hook is never miscounted) and carries **both controls**: a negative control proving
`EffectiveFeaturesProvider.tsx`'s docblock — which literally contains
`useEffectiveFeatures(user?.id ?? null)` — is not counted, and a positive control proving a
planted call site *is* found.

### Falsifications (every guard broken for real, then reverted)

| GAP-2 | `WorkflowsPage.tsx`: a real second call site planted (`import` + `const __leak = useEffectiveFeatures(null)`) | **2 red** — `expected 1 to be +0` on the band-owners guard; `expected 2 to be 1` on the positive control | ✅ 32/32 |
