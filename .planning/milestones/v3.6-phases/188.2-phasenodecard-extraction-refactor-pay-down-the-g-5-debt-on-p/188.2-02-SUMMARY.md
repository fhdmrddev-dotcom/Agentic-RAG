## ⚠ BOARD B's DRIVEN ROW IS STILL OWED — this plan closes the UNIT half only

Stated plainly, as `<output>` requires: **no test written in this plan can see the defect it fixes.** jsdom applies no CSS, computes no stacking contexts and lays nothing out; `.click()` and `fireEvent.click()` dispatch straight to the element and bypass hit-testing entirely. That is exactly why `BUG-260806-01` survived from 184-12 until an operator found it by hand during 188.1's UAT with every one of these 58 tests green.

What this plan shipped is the half that **re-runs on every build**: the relation, the library's elevation, and the wiring. What it did **not** ship, and what remains owed:

> **A driven `document.elementFromPoint` row at the `✕`'s own centre, with the card SELECTED, returning the `✕` and not the card — driven in Plan 07 against Board B.**

Per D-11 the defect is only guarded once both halves exist. A driven row alone is a one-time photograph; a unit assertion alone cannot see the pixel. Neither is sufficient, and this plan delivered one of the two.

**No browser evidence was obtained in this plan**, and none was attempted — the plan does not call for it here, and substituting a unit test as proof of hit-testing is precisely the substitution that let this bug ship. Plan 07 owns the driven row.
