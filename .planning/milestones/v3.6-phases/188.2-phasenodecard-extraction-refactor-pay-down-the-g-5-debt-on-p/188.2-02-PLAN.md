<objective>
Close `BUG-260806-01`: a **selected** phase card paints over its own `✕` remove control, so the
natural flow — click the phase you want to delete, then click its `✕` — is not completable with
a real pointer.

Purpose: this is the phase's SECOND contract (D-10), and stating it up front is the point.
`188.2` proves the card UNCHANGED; this plan proves the affordance FIXED. Precedent is
`188.1-04`, which carried three bounded security fixes as its own plan for the same "sits in
exactly this subtree" reason. G-3 would otherwise size this `/gsd:fast` (≤ 1 file, ≤ 10 lines,
no schema or API surface) — D-10 overrides that with a recorded reason, and the plan must not
silently re-derive G-3.

The mechanism is traced end to end from the installed package's own source, not hypothesised:
`@xyflow/system/dist/esm/index.js:1547` declares `SELECTED_NODE_Z = 1000`; it is applied as an
**inline style** at `@xyflow/react/dist/esm/index.js:2343`. `.react-flow__nodes` carries no
`position` and no `z-index` (`base.css:174-177`) so it creates **no stacking context**, and each
`.react-flow__node` therefore competes directly inside the viewport's context at its own inline
z. `.react-flow__viewport-portal` has no `z-index` at all (`base.css:301`) and is the LAST
sibling. Deselected: node `0` vs portal `auto`, tie on z, later in DOM order wins ⇒ the `✕` is
reachable. Selected: node `1000` beats `auto` ⇒ the card paints over the `✕`. That derivation
reproduces the report's measured hit-test table exactly.

The fix is one property on the affordance elements — not a change to the portal container, and
certainly not to the card. **All three groups are fixed, not just the `✕`:** the report shows
the `＋` buttons reachable only because no node adjacent to them happened to be selected.

Output: two named constants, three inline `zIndex` properties, a three-part unit guard that
re-runs every build, and one authorised baseline re-capture.
</objective>
