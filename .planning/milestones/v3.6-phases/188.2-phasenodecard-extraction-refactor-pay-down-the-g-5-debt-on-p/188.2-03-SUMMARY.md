## Decisions Made

1. **`CARD_SHAPE` captures eleven named attributes, not five.** The plan named `testid`, `class`, `style`, `ariaHidden`, `ariaLabel`. Measured, that set makes the matrix blind to the very thing C-6 is supposed to catch: the arc's whole geometry reaches the DOM as SVG **presentation attributes** (`r`, `stroke-dasharray`, `stroke-dashoffset`, `stroke`, `stroke-width`, `stroke-linecap`), never through `class` or `style`. On class+style alone the seven readings capture as **three** distinct arc signatures (no arc / the spin utility / five identical). All eleven are read with `getAttribute`, so the plan's load-bearing "a missing attribute captures as `null`" property is preserved for every one of them. The finding is pinned as its own live assertion (`expect(new Set(classStyleOnly).size).toBe(3)`) so nobody later narrows the reducer back believing five was ever enough.
2. **A third literal, `CARD_BORDER_SHAPES`** — see Deviations, Rule 2.
3. **`CARD_SHAPE_BASELINE` is an aggregate of references, not a fourth literal.** The plan's frontmatter names the artifact `CARD_SHAPE_BASELINE`; its `<action>` and success criteria name `CARD_READING_SHAPES` / `CARD_VERDICT_SHAPES`. Both are honoured. Holding the same references (asserted with `toBe`, not `toEqual`) is what stops the aggregate going stale while every assertion above stays green.
4. **The marker rows assert over the COMMITTED baseline strings, not a fresh render.** The threat they answer (T-188.2-11) is "a capture that rendered nothing and passes forever", which is a claim about what was captured.
5. **The card div is filed under a synthetic key**, `"(card-div — the 137-B card, no data-testid)"`. The leading parenthesis keeps a stable sort position and cannot be mistaken for a real test id.
6. **`CARD_HTML_ROWS` and `CARD_BORDER_ROWS` declare each row's props once**, shared by the capture and by the assertion, so the props a baseline was captured from and the props an assertion renders cannot drift apart.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed / auto-added

**1. [Rule 2 — Missing critical functionality] `CARD_BORDER_SHAPES` added as a third literal**

- **Found during:** Task 2, while measuring what the plan's two literals actually cover.
- **Issue:** This plan's own `must_haves.truths` require the capture to reach *"the card div's four border branches"*. The plan's two literals reach **two**: the reading matrix passes neither `selected` nor `grounded`, and the three HTML rows are `run` (MAXIMAL_RUNNING / MAXIMAL_WAITING) and `default` (MINIMAL_BUILDER). The `selected` and `grounded` branches would have been captured by nothing at all.
- **Fix:** A third `Record<string, ReturnType<typeof CARD_SHAPE>>` over four rows whose props deliberately OVERLAP (`run` also passes `selected` and `grounded`; `selected` also passes `grounded`), so the four captures pin the **precedence order** `run > selected > grounded > default` and not merely four strings. A companion row asserts the four card-div class lists are pairwise distinct — proof the ternary really has four live arms.
