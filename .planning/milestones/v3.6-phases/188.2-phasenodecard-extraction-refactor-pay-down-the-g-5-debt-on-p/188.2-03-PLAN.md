<objective>
Capture the card's rendered shape from the shipped tree and commit it as a baseline, so the
cut has something to be identical *to*.

Purpose: the phase's first contract is "the card renders exactly what it rendered". The cheap
wrong way to check that is to hand-type the expected attributes, which proves only what the
author believed the geometry was. The house instrument (`WorkflowCanvas.editing.test.tsx:1183-1234`,
shipped by 188.1-02) is to CAPTURE the DOM from the tree as it ships and assert the post-move
tree reproduces it byte for byte.

**The baseline must PREDATE the change.** That is not a preference: it is one of 188.1's nine
measured-false inherited claims, and it is why this is a wave of its own, before any destination
file exists. A baseline captured after the edit proves the edit against itself.

Two instruments, because they fail differently. `CARD_HTML_BASELINE` pins whole-`innerHTML`
byte-identity for three maximal/minimal rows — the strongest possible statement, and the shipped
idiom at `PhaseNodeCard.test.tsx:302-310`. `CARD_SHAPE_BASELINE` pins per-element class and style
across the seven readings and the three verdicts — compact, reviewable, and the instrument whose
failure output names WHICH element moved.

Output: both baselines, a determinism proof across two agreeing runs, and a RED observation
against a one-value geometry change.
</objective>
