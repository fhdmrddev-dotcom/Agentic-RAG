<objective>
Render the server's verdicts — on the node face as a small corner mark, and in a bottom tray that has a
home for the ones belonging to no node — without the client deciding anything about them.

Purpose: VALID-03 says per-node status comes from the server verdict and never from a client guess.
That is a flexibility rule as much as a correctness one: future governance ships as new verdict codes in
the owning backend module and the canvas renders whatever comes back, keyed by `verdict.phase`, with no
canvas change at all. R9 is the harder half — both severities set `ok:false` and both block publish, but
only one is bad news, and a surface that paints them the same tells a person they have done something
wrong every time they pause halfway.

Output: a pure grouping module, the two node marks, and the problems tray. Nothing is mounted on the
canvas yet — 184-13 composes the bottom region.
