<objective>
Stop the ⌥ reveal from destroying the plain title, on both graph views.

Purpose: the shipped reveal is a swap — `title = technical ? technicalTitle : title`. That was cheap
when the plain title was the generic "Work out how to do it"; the derived tier makes it specific, so
the same swap now destroys real meaning. It also truncates the one thing the reveal exists to show:
the card title is `truncate` at 14px in a 248px card, so `technicalTitle` renders as
`AI agent step · find-renewal-t…` and the **slug clips**. The subtitle slot is not truncated and wraps,
so moving the technical string there puts the full slug in the DOM.

Output: the canvas subtitle swap with its render tests, and the spine's title agreement.
</objective>
