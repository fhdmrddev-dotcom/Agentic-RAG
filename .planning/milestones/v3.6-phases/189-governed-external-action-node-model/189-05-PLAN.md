Resolve CONFLICT 1 (decision D-19): stop the always-armed checkpoint from killing a publish, without
loosening the arming.

Purpose: `189-RESEARCH.md` measured the whole chain. Stage 2.5 blocks a publish pre-run for exactly two
shapes — an `llm_human_input` phase, and a validator whose `on_failure` is `ask_user`. The armed

stands, and plan 189-02 proved it with a driven test rather than a reading.

class as `BUG-260731-02`, which is why migration 114 exists. It must be proven DRIVEN, never asserted.

Output: `is_golden_run` threaded onto the ctx, one branch at the checkpoint, and 189-02's CONFLICT-1
RED going green with both controls intact.
