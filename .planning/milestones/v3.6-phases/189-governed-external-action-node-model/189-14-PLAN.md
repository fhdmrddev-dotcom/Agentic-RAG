<objective>
Give the author a way to choose a capability, and make the arming switch tell the truth about being
unmovable.

Purpose: two constraints shape this plan and both are recorded rather than invented.

First, the side panel is on the hot-file ledger, and its row says verbatim: *"Keep this shape — the next
surface that needs the panel gets its own component and one gated line."* Phase 185 added an entire
governance feature to that panel for four insertions in the render body, with everything else living in
its own component. **189 is that next surface, and a plan that grows the panel with a block of picker
JSX violates a ledger row.**

Second, D-04 says the arming switch cannot be turned off on this type. A control that can be pressed and
changes nothing is a lie — so it renders ON and non-interactive with its reason as real DOM text.

Output: one new component and its suite, one gated line, one refusal constant, one refusing switch, and
a cross-language fence that makes the client mirror mechanically honest.
</objective>
