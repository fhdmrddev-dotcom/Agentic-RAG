- **Camelot wins** — recommended per bench data + operator direction; 15 vs 9 on friendly_real and 214 vs 188 on thesis. Runtime (1.27s/21.10s) competitive with pymupdf's (3.56s/19.86s).

The ROADMAP SC#6 ship floor (≥15/≥12 rendered tables) is decided by Plan 05's UAT scoreboard against rendered output, not Plan 01's raw counts. WINNER.md records both floors explicitly so the distinction is durable.
- **Thesis re-run scope** — pymupdf + camelot only (per user); did NOT spend time downgrading transformers to get a gmft number, because the dep chain disqualifies gmft regardless.
