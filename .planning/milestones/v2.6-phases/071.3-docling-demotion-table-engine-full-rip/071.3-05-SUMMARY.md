- **Fix:** Explicit MIME in curl form parameter: `-F "file=@reference.docx;type=application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document"`.
- **Files modified:** none (no code change — operator-side workaround)

## Issues Encountered

### Camelot raw output is false-positive heavy

The thesis fixture returns 214 tables vs the 35-table ground truth — a 6.1x over-count. Per WINNER.md's note, this was expected (camelot's `Stream` mode detects tabular-looking regions including figure captions, multi-column text blocks, and narrow text columns). Per D-071.3-17, "any detection above bbox-area threshold counts" — structural fidelity is not a gating metric. Downstream `query_tables` retrieval will surface the best matches by ranking. If post-ship user reports surface specific *missing* tables (not just noise), SEED-021's trigger #1 fires.

Not blocking the phase close, but worth flagging in carry-forward observations for future retrieval audits (SEED-020 would also surface this if low-quality tables impact chat-answer quality).

### Image recall stuck at 20 across both PDFs

The `pymupdf_full` adapter found 20 images on the thesis (pre-046 baseline was also 30, post-046 is 20 — actually a slight regression depending on the comparison point) and 1 image on the small arXiv fixture. This is the headline gap driving the SEED-021 plant on the image axis. Not a Plan 05 issue — it's the residue of D-071.3-04's scope decision ("071.3 is tables-focused"). Documented in carry-forward observations in HUMAN-UAT.md and in SEED-021's plant body.
