## Accomplishments

- **Backend (Task 1):** The POST `/threads/{tid}/messages` dispatch `JSONResponse` now carries additive `model`/`provider` — the already-resolved `_resolved_model`/`_resolved_provider` (computed at `threads.py:921-954` before `insert_run`, and written to the `runs` row). ## Decisions Made

- **Additive, not re-resolved (Task 1):** surfaced the already-computed `_resolved_model`/`_resolved_provider` rather than re-deriving on the client — the dispatch response and the runs row (hence the reload enrich) are guaranteed identical.
