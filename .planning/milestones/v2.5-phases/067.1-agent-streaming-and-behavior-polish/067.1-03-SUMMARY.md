Tightening below 200 tokens by removing any of these would break acceptance. - **Positive framing only.** No negative directive (e.g. "Do NOT ask for confirmation") — per Anthropic published prompt-engineering guidance referenced in RESEARCH § Anti-Patterns. The "If you want, I can also..." phrases appear only as labelled anti-examples inside the positive directive, not as prohibitions.
- **EXCEPTIONS as a sibling sub-section, not a bullet inside the directive.** Preserves the V11 destructive-autonomy guardrail as an explicit and easily-auditable carve-out (per threat model T-067.1-03). Future PRs adding new clarification cases (e.g. PII-handling) extend the EXCEPTIONS list rather than re-flipping the autonomy default.
