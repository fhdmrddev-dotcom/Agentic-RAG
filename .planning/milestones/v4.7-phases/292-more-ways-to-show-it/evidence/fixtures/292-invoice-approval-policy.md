# Invoice Approval Policy (fixture, generated for Phase 292 UAT)

Document owner: Finance Operations. Applies to every supplier invoice received by the company.
This is a generated test document; it contains no real supplier, person or amount.

## Purpose

This policy describes how a supplier invoice moves from receipt to payment, who approves it, and
what happens when an invoice is wrong. Every invoice follows the same steps in the same order.

## The approval process, step by step

1. **Invoice received.** The accounts payable inbox receives the supplier invoice and logs it in the
   payables register on the same working day.
2. **Three-way match check.** Accounts payable checks that the invoice matches the purchase order and
   the goods receipt note: same supplier, same quantities, same unit prices.
3. **Decision: does the invoice match the purchase order?** If it matches, the invoice goes to the
   budget holder for approval. If it does not match, the invoice is rejected and returned to the
   supplier.
4. **Rejected: return to the supplier.** Accounts payable emails the supplier the reason for the
   rejection. The supplier corrects the invoice and resubmits it, and the resubmitted invoice goes
   back to the three-way match check. A rejected invoice is never paid.
5. **Budget holder approval.** The budget holder for the cost centre approves the invoice within five
   working days.
6. **Finance controller sign-off.** Any invoice above 10,000 euros also needs the finance controller's
   sign-off after the budget holder has approved it.
7. **Payment scheduled.** Accounts payable schedules the approved invoice for the next weekly
   payment run.
8. **Paid.** Treasury releases the payment on the Thursday payment run.
9. **Filed for audit.** The paid invoice, its purchase order and its approvals are filed in the audit
   archive for seven years.

## The approver chain

The approver chain is: accounts payable clerk, then the budget holder, then the finance controller
(above 10,000 euros only), then treasury for release. Nobody may approve an invoice they raised.

## Escalation

If the budget holder has not approved an invoice within five working days, accounts payable sends a
reminder. After a second reminder the invoice is escalated to the head of finance.
