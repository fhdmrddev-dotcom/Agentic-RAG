and make sure tool-call markup is never saved or shown as an answer, for every provider.

ever saved or shown with raw markup). This is the core fix for BUG-261024-03: causes D-01 (strip
gated on `deepseek`), D-02 (wrong opener literal) and D-04 (cap check reads only the structured
buffer), with the parser D-03 says does not exist yet.

marker filter in `db/runs.py`, the retired deepseek-only strip in `openai_compat.py`, and the tests.
