## Decisions Made
- **Timer lives in `_run_arm_body`, not `_run_arm`.** The plan's `<interfaces>` cited `_run_arm` (:553-565) for the await, but the shipped code has since split into `_run_arm` (heartbeat pulse shell) + `_run_arm_body` (the loop/grade/persist body).
