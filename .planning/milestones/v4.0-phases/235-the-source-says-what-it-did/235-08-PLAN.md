<objective>
Make the Library Health tab reachable from outside the Library — because it is not, today, and SURF-03's whole
route (badge → popover → Health → card) is impossible until it is.

Purpose: `LibraryPage`'s signature is `({ onNavigate })` and nothing else. The tab is
`useReducer(pageReducer, initialLibraryState)` internal state whose initial value hardcodes
`{ tab: "documents" }`, and the only way to change it is a `SELECT_TAB` dispatch from one of three call sites,
all inside `LibraryPage`. This plan threads a net-new `initialTab` down the shipped
`studioSkillId`/`studioTab` path — the precedent to copy, not a second shape to invent — and adds the
Health→Ingestion hop the Health row will use.

Output: `App.tsx` holds the tab beside `activeView` with ONE navigator that sets both; `ChatLayout` forwards it;
`LibraryPage` composes it at the reducer boundary and gains a `go to source` handler.
</objective>
