## The blocker — CONN-02, stated precisely

The requirement's words are *"2–3 first-party **live** connectors ... runnable **from a workflow**"*.
Three adapters shipped. **One is reachable from a workflow, and it works by coincidence.**

`_adapter_args` (`phase_types.py:1985-2005`) populates exactly **one** field — the capability's
declared `body_arg`. Against each adapter's own required-argument list:

| Capability | Adapter requires | `_adapter_args` supplies | Reachable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `post_message` (Slack) | `["text"]` — `slack_adapter.py:372` | `text` (it IS the body_arg) | ✅ **yes — by coincidence** |
| `create_ticket` (Jira) | `["summary","description"]` — `jira_adapter.py:422` | `description` only | ❌ **no** — `summary` unfillable |
| `send_email` (SMTP) | `["to","subject","body"]` — `smtp_adapter.py:296` | `body` only | ❌ **no** — `to`/`subject` unfillable |

`ExternalActionPhaseConfig` (`harness.py:242`) has no field for `summary`, `to` or `subject`, and
neither does `ExternalActionSection` or `ConnectionPicker`. Both paths raise an adapter argument
error at `phase_types.py:2318-2332`, which is caught and reported as `failed`. **A real ticket or
email cannot be produced from a workflow, by construction.**

**Why this is a phase, not a patch.** The missing piece is an author-facing surface — schema
fields, form controls, serializer, and the validation that goes with them — plus a decision about
whether those fields are per-phase config or belong on the connection (which is exactly what
`SEED-144` / `SEED-145` / `SEED-146` re-opened). Building it inside a closure round would be
adding a user-facing capability to a closure round, which **G-7 forbids in as many words**.

**Second-order usability trap** (warning, not blocker): an author who binds a Jira or email
connection today gets an ordinary-looking `failed` phase carrying the vendor's argument-validation
error, with nothing telling them the phase type structurally cannot supply those fields.
