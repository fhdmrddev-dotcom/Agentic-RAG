# Trigger Tuner — Reconciled Fix Backlog

> **Key correction:** the original live-debug hypothesis (the `anyOf` nullable schema) was
> **refuted as the PRIMARY trigger** by all three adversarial lenses. The real blocker is the
> **wrong tool-spec SHAPE** (`_emit_tool` emits the Anthropic top-level shape, not the canonical
> OpenAI `{type:"function", function:{…}}`). `anyOf` is a real but **secondary/latent** trap that
> only bites once the wrapper is fixed.

## 1. Executive summary

The Trigger Tuner benchmark does **not** currently work cross-provider: it silently misfires on every native-SDK provider (Anthropic, Google) and hard-400s on the strict OpenAI-compatible validators (moonshot, minimax). The **single root blocker** is a malformed tool-spec shape: `skill_tuner_service._emit_tool` (line 140) emits the Anthropic-native top-level shape `{name, description, input_schema}` instead of the canonical OpenAI shape `{type:"function", function:{name, description, parameters}}` that every other working `forced_emit` caller uses, and the openai-compat adapter ships it to the wire verbatim with no normalization. Everything else — the redis_timeout, the frozen "queued" lanes, the scary "lost connection" terminal, the LangSmith 429 flood — is either a downstream symptom of this storm or a separate UX/observability polish item that does not, by itself, break the benchmark.
