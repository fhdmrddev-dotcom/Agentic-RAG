Close CR-02 (the GATE-01 citation_policy non-strict delivery blocker) plus the coupled WR-06 and IN-03.

Today the non-strict `citation_policy` (flag/partial/draft) path in `_exec_llm_emit` applies `apply_citation_policy`, mutates `legacy_map`, then falls through to the render dispatch — but `tool_dispatcher._handle_render_template` re-runs `check_coverage` and HARD-rejects any map with `uncited_value_count > 0 OR invented_citation_count > 0`. Strict stays byte-identical (no `citation_policy_applied` set → the gate rejects exactly as today). Fold in WR-06 (match invented + full-leaf pair; fail back to strict when nothing was modified) and IN-03 (surface the policy summary only AFTER a successful render).

Purpose: GATE-01's `citation_policy` is one of the SEED-082 engine seams 103 surfaces with one plain-language question — flag/partial/draft must actually deliver (marked/blanked/labeled), never silently rejected nor falsely reported delivered.
Output: A policy-aware render gate across phase_types/emitters/tool_dispatcher; a leaf-accurate emit_policy; honest single-surface ordering; one UN-MOCKED render round-trip test driving the real `_handle_render_template` gate.
