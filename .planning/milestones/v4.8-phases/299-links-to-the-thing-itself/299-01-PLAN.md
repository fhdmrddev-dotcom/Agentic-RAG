Two measured facts change RESEARCH Pattern 2's "every kind on get_user_pg_connection". Read at

P1 — RUN. The workflow_runs SELECT policy is OWNER-ONLY: `workflow_runs_select_own ... USING (org_id IN
current_user_org_ids() AND auth.uid() = (SELECT threads.user_id ...))` (supabase/full-schema.sql
~:10238). On the user-JWT connection the D-34 org-member arm can NEVER return a row, so a finished
org run the evidence report shows would read as "can't be opened". The shipped evidence access
query runs on the SERVICE POOL with the caller bound explicitly (`workflow_run_evidence.py` `_ACCESS_SQL`,
`wr.user_id = $2 OR EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM org_members m WHERE m.org_id = wr.org_id AND m.user_id = $2)`).
The run kind copies that posture.

P2 — EXPERT. `expert_bundles_read_policy` for `authenticated` is `is_system OR (org member AND (visibility
IN ('org','public') OR created_by = auth.uid()))` (~:9506) — there is NO granted arm, and
`api/experts.py:get_expert` reads through the pool (`check_expert_grant_access(pool, ...)`). On the
user-JWT connection a `granted` Expert would read as "can't be opened" to its grantee. The Expert kind
runs on the pool with `$2` = caller; the experts:manage arm is the inline body of
`current_user_has_permission` (`org_members m JOIN role_permissions rp ON rp.role = m.role WHERE
m.org_id = b.org_id AND m.user_id = $2 AND rp.permission_key = 'experts:manage'`), because
`auth.uid()` is NULL on the pool.

Thread and workflow keep the RLS posture (`get_user_pg_connection`): their RLS policies ARE their read
rules (`"Users can view their own threads"` ~:9117; workflow_definitions ~:8968). Within each kind
missing and forbidden still take ONE statement on ONE connection type, which is what D-08 requires.
