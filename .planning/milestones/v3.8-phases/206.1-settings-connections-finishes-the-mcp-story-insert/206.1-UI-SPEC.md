## Spacing Scale

The shipped rhythm of this surface, re-derived from the source rather than proposed. A dense row
that invents a new step is a row that will not sit flush with the wide one.

| Token | Value | Tailwind | Usage on this surface |
|-------|-------|----------|-----------------------|
| 2xs | 4px | `gap-1`, `mb-1`, `gap-y-1` | label→value, help→control, dense line gutters |
| xs | 6px | `gap-1.5`, `mt-1.5`, `py-1.5` | mark→text inside the destination cell `[SHIPPED]`; the chooser `<select>`'s vertical padding `[SHIPPED, untouched]`; dense line-to-line gap `[NEW — exception 4]` |
| sm | 8px | `gap-2`, `mt-2` | mark→name; button clusters |
| md | 12px | `gap-x-3`, `px-3`, `py-3`, `mt-3` | inter-column gap; row vertical padding; footer cluster |
| lg | 16px | `gap-4`, `px-4`, `mt-4` | the split-track gutter (`connections-split`), sheet padding |
| xl | 24px | `p-6`, `py-6` | `CardContent` padding (shadcn default, untouched) |
| 2xl | 48px | `py-12` | the two empty states' vertical breathing |

**Declared exceptions (the first three are INHERITED and NOT extended; the fourth IS extended by
this phase, which is why it is written down separately rather than passed off as an ordinary token):**

1. **`px-3.5` / `mb-3.5` = 14px** — the shipped table-row horizontal padding and the shipped
   field-to-field rhythm inside the panel. `ConnectionFormPanel.tsx:274` names it in source as
   *"the SHIPPED panel rhythm, an inherited exception (§11a)"*. `[SHIPPED]` The dense row **reuses
   `px-3.5 py-3` unchanged**, so the two shapes share one outer box.
2. **`w-24` / `w-32` / `w-36` / `w-8` (96 / 128 / 144 / 32px)** — the wide shape's four
   non-shrinkable column widths. `[RESEARCH §OQ#3]` These exist **only in the wide shape** and are
   absent from the dense shape by construction (that is the point of the reflow).
3. **⋯ touch target `h-7 w-7` = 28px, below the 44px guideline.** Shipped, and **this phase must
   not change it** — the wide row's markup is asserted byte-identical (SC#2a). The dense shape
   reuses the identical button. `[SHIPPED]` Re-open trigger: *the first phase permitted to
   re-baseline `ConnectionsTab.test.tsx`'s wide-render capture.*

4. **`gap-1.5` = 6px, and ⚠ THIS ONE IS EXTENDED — it is the only spacing decision this phase
   actually makes.** 6px is neither a multiple of 4 nor a member of the standard set
   {4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64}. ⚠ **CORRECTED 2026-08-25 by plan `206.1-02`, measured — the original
   sentence is kept below rather than overwritten, because the DECISION it justifies is unchanged
   and only its supporting count was wrong.** A whole-file grep for `gap-1.5` reads **7**, not 2, and
   the token ships at **FOUR** kinds of site, not one. That does not weaken exception 4 — the
   *dense container* is still a new kind of site, and 6px is still off the 4px grid — but a reader
   checking the claim against the source would have found it false, and a spacing exception whose
   own evidence does not reproduce is worth less than no exception at all.
   Original, preserved: *"It ships today at ONE kind of site — mark→text *inside* the
   destination cell (`ConnectionsTab.tsx`'s `gap-1.5` on the `Sends to` cell) — and this phase puts
   it at a SECOND, structurally different one: the dense row's own container
   (`flex flex-col gap-1.5 px-3.5 py-3`), spacing three stacked LINES apart. Splitting the two uses
   here rather than letting the table imply one is the point of this entry.
   **Justification for the new use, in one sentence:** at a 302px track the three dense lines must
   read as ONE row, and 8px (`gap-2`) is the row's own inter-column gap — reusing it makes the lines
   read as gapped siblings rather than as a stack, while 4px (`gap-1`) is the label→value step and
   collides the lines' baselines at 11px/13px. `[AUTO-RESOLVED — this pass]`"* (end of preserved original)
   Re-open trigger: *the first phase that needs a fourth dense line, or that puts a dense row on a
   track wider than ~400px* — both change which gap reads as "one row".
   ⚠ It is extended to the dense CONTAINER and nowhere else. `gap-1.5` may not appear on any new
   node outside that container and the shipped destination cell.

**Forbidden:** any new spacing value not in the table above. In particular the dense shape may not
introduce `py-2.5`, `gap-2.5` or `px-2` on the row — a second rhythm is how two shapes of one row
start looking like two components.

## Typography

**The whole surface ships exactly TWO sizes.** Measured this pass across both files:
`text-[11px]` × 53 and `text-[13px]` × 44, and **no other `text-[Npx]` at all**.
`[MEASURED-HERE]` That is a stricter contract than the 3-4 the checker asks for, and it is
deliberate: an instrument table with three type sizes stops being scannable.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height | Where |
|------|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Body** | 13px | 500 (`font-medium`) | `leading-relaxed` ≈ 1.6 for prose; default ≈1.5 in cells | the connection NAME, the chooser `<select>`, every text input, the capability's static kind, Save/Cancel labels, sheet body copy |
| **Meta / Label** | 11px | 500 (`font-medium`) for labels & state; 400 for values | `leading-snug` ≈ 1.4 | column headers, field labels, help lines, `Sends to`, `Used by`, `Credential`, the state word, filter chips, the live count, receipts, refusal notices |
| Heading | **not used** | — | — | the surface has no heading larger than Body. `connection-form-title` is `text-[13px] font-semibold` |
| Display | **not used** | — | — | no hero, no summary tiles, no chart (`ConnectionsTab.tsx` header, §2c) |

**Weights: exactly two are authorable by this phase — 400 (regular) and 500 (`font-medium`).**
`font-semibold` (600) exists on this surface at **one node only** — the panel title
(`ConnectionFormPanel.tsx:771`) `[MEASURED-HERE]` — and is an inherited exception this phase must
not extend. Nothing new is semibold, nothing anywhere is bold.

**`font-mono` (JetBrains Mono) is a ROLE, not a decoration**, and its membership is closed:
machine-shaped values only — the destination (`Sends to`), the credential reading, the live count,
the Jira project key input, the SMTP port input, the kill-switch flag `<code>`, the secret dots.
**The new MCP server URL input is `font-mono`** (it is a machine value, exactly like the Jira site
field's sibling). **The MCP field's LABEL and HELP are not** — labels are prose.

## Color

Deep Midnight is the lived-in theme; both blocks are declared because every state word must read
in either. Values are the CSS variables, not literals — **no hex is ever written in a component.**

| Role | Token | Light | Dark (Deep Midnight) | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|----------------------|-------|
| **Dominant (60%)** | `--background` / `--card` | `220 20% 97%` / `0 0% 100%` | `216 45% 4%` / `220 30% 7%` | the page canvas and the table's card surface; rows are transparent over it |
| **Secondary (30%)** | `--muted` at low alpha, and `--border` | `220 14% 94%` / `220 13% 89%` | `220 30% 11%` / `220 20% 16%` | the header strip and disabled rows (`bg-muted/20`), `divide-y divide-border/60` row rules, the panel track's edge, input and chip borders |
| **Accent (10%)** | `--primary` | `239 84% 67%` | `239 100% 82%` | see the reserved list below |
| **Destructive** | `--destructive` | `0 84% 60%` | `0 72% 51%` | see the reserved list below |
| Semantic — success | `--success` | `142 71% 45%` | `142 71% 45%` | `✓ Ready` state word; the receipt's check glyph |
| Semantic — warning | `--warning` | `38 92% 60%` | `38 92% 60%` | `◌ Not checked` state word; the `live_connectors` OFF banner |
| Muted text | `--muted-foreground` | `220 9% 46%` | `220 16% 65%` | every 11px value; the dense inline labels |

**Accent (`--primary`) is reserved for EXACTLY these elements and nothing else:**

1. The **Save** button's fill (`bg-primary text-primary-foreground`) — the one filled control.
2. The **focus ring** (`--ring`, which equals `--primary`) and the chooser `<select>`'s
   `focus:border-primary`.
3. The **pressed filter chip** (`border-primary/40 bg-primary/15 text-primary`).

Not links, not the row name (`hover:underline`, no colour change), not the mark, not the state
words, not the ⋯. A fourth accent site in this phase is a contract violation.

**Destructive is reserved for EXACTLY these elements:**

1. The Delete / Disable confirm sheets' confirm button and the panel's delete body.
2. The cipher-unavailable notice **and the disabled-Save reason paragraph** — including the new
   MCP one (see § Surface 1).
3. The egress-refusal notice on the save path.
4. The `✕ Credential failed` state word and the `Couldn't save that` / `Couldn't apply that change`
   lines.

## Surface 2 — the DENSE row shape in `ConnectionsTab.tsx`

### How it turns on

⚠ **A `dense` prop threaded from the SAME expression that opens the 400px track** — one condition,
one home, two consequences. `[CONTEXT D-206.1-12]`

```tsx
// ConnectionsTab.tsx:274 — the ONE condition
const dense = Boolean(panel) && !isMobile
// gridTemplateColumns: dense ? "minmax(0,1fr) 400px" : "minmax(0,1fr)"
```

Container queries are **refused and not revisited**: no `@tailwindcss/container-queries` is
installed (a second new dependency), and jsdom evaluates no layout, so a container-driven shape
would be untestable by the suite that has to pin it.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Row attribute | `data-dense={dense ? "true" : "false"}` — **always present, both values**. An absent attribute reading as `false` is the "0 is a fact, absence is a different one" trap |
| Column header | **NOT rendered in dense.** `CONNECTIONS_COLUMNS` stays exported and stays rendered by the wide shape, character-identity intact. The dense cells do not form five aligned columns, so a five-word header would label a grid that is not there. `[CONTEXT D-206.1-20]` Assert `queryByTestId("connections-header")` **null in dense, present in wide** |
| Wide shape | **byte-identical to base** — captured as `outerHTML` before the change and compared after (the `PhaseNodeCard.tsx` `CARD_HTML_BASELINE` precedent: a pin that passes UNEDITED is evidence; a re-captured one is only a record) `[VALIDATION SC#2a]` |

### The chosen layout — **three lines, ⋯ on line 1**

> **PRIMARY VISUAL ANCHOR — stated once, plainly, because every layout decision below is scored
> against it.** The row's anchor is **the connection's NAME** — the author's own word, at 13px/500,
> first in DOM order and first on line 1 in BOTH shapes. It is the thing a person looks for when
> they scan the table (`ConnectionsTab.tsx`'s own header: *"A person landing here looks at rows, and
> the first thing any row says is its NAME"*). Nothing added by this phase — not the mark, not the
> state word, not the ⋯ — may take width or visual weight from it. **Second in the hierarchy is the
> destination**, because it is the one column a person reads to approve a send; that is why it gets
> a full line to itself in the dense shape rather than a share of one.

RESEARCH offered three (A two-line-plus-meta, B destination-alone, C `grid-cols-[1fr_auto]`).
**This spec locks a fourth that is B's line order with A's action placement**, and here is why each
of the three was not taken as written:

- **Not A as written.** A puts the state word on line 1 beside the name. At a 302px track the fixed
  costs on that line are `⏻ Disabled`…`✕ Credential failed` (≈105px at 11px mono-ish), ⋯ (32px),
  gaps (16px), padding (28px) — leaving the name **≈110px**, which truncates most real names. The
  name is the author's own word and the row's primary anchor; squeezing it to buy a line is the
  wrong trade on the one surface whose defect *is* squeezing.
- **Not C.** `row-span-3` pins ⋯ at `self-start`, i.e. **above** the vertical centre of the name —
  a moved hit target on a control that is already 28px, on a shape nobody has used before.
- **Not B as written.** B right-aligns ⋯ on line 3, which moves it further still and puts the row's
  only destructive door at the bottom of a variable-height block.

**Locked dense shape:**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  ← px-3.5 py-3, flex flex-col gap-1.5
│ [◆] Northwind Jira                           ⋯  │  line 1  mark · name · action|receipt
│ 🔒 northwind.atlassian.net · NW                 │  line 2  destination — WRAPS
│ ✓ Ready   Used by 2 steps   Credential 3d ago   │  line 3  state · labelled meta
```

|---|---|---|
| container | `flex flex-col gap-1.5 px-3.5 py-3` + `!is_enabled && "bg-muted/20"` | — |
| **1** | `flex items-center gap-2` | mark `h-4 w-4 flex-none` (or the `•` fallback span, unchanged) · `connections-row-name` `flex-1 min-w-0 truncate text-[13px] font-medium text-foreground` · action cell `flex min-w-[2rem] flex-none items-center justify-end` holding `connections-row-more` **or** `connections-receipt` (the shipped either/or) |

### ⚠ Line 2 is where SC#2 is won or lost

The destination cell is `truncate` today = `overflow:hidden; text-overflow:ellipsis;
**white-space:nowrap**`. With `nowrap`, `scrollWidth` is the full un-wrapped text width — the
operator measured `scrollW 272 / clientW 93` and `169 / 47`. **`scrollWidth <= clientWidth` is
therefore UNSATISFIABLE BY WIDENING ALONE.** `[CONTEXT D-206.1-21]`

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| The dense destination cell's class list contains **no `truncate`** | asserted directly (SC#2a) |
| The value span is `whitespace-normal break-all` | `break-all`, not `break-words`: a URL has no spaces, so `break-words` will not break it |
| The rendered `white-space` is not `nowrap` | asserted in the browser tier alongside the geometry — `cellFits: true` **while** `nowrap` means a short fixture was measured, not a working shape |
| ⚠ A `title` tooltip | **FORBIDDEN.** `ConnectionsTab.test.tsx:505-515` asserts **zero** `[title]` nodes, including with a menu and a sheet open. Wrapping is the answer *because* the tooltip is not available |

### ⚠ All five columns and the ⋯ are present in BOTH shapes

**Deleting a column is the ROADMAP's named failure mode** — *"the destination is the one column a
person reads to approve a send, and hiding it is worse than truncating it."* `[CONTEXT D-206.1-13]`

Assert by querying **every** row testid in **both** renders, not by inspection:
`connections-row-name` · `connections-row-destination` · `connections-row-usedby` ·
`connections-row-credential` · `connections-row-state` · `connections-row-more`.

`connections-row-usedby` and `connections-row-credential` lose their column header in dense, so each
gains a **visible inline label**:

```tsx

  <span className="text-muted-foreground">{CONNECTIONS_DENSE_LABEL_USED_BY}</span>
  <span data-testid="connections-row-usedby" className="text-muted-foreground">{usedByLabel(usedBy)}</span>
```

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| ⚠ **The label is a SIBLING node, never inside the testid'd node** | `ConnectionsTab.test.tsx:640` asserts `connections-row-credential`'s textContent `.toBe("never checked")` by **exact equality**, and `:641` anchors `/^checked /` at the start. Keeping the label outside means **the value node's textContent is character-identical in BOTH shapes**, so the same assertion holds for dense too. Putting the label inside would force a re-baseline — of a pin, on the surface whose whole lesson is that re-baselined pins are not evidence |
| The label is **not** `aria-hidden` | it carries meaning for AT users exactly as for sighted ones; the reading becomes "Credential checked 3d ago" |
| The label is `text-muted-foreground` | **not** `text-muted-foreground-dim` — that class compiles to nothing (see § Color) |
| The label text is **derived** from `CONNECTIONS_COLUMNS[2]` / `[3]` | the header and the inline label are structurally unable to disagree |

`connections-row-state` needs **no** label — glyph + word are self-describing.
`connections-row-destination` needs **no** label — the 🔒 already leads it, and it is the only
`font-mono` line.

### Height cost, stated so it is a decision and not a discovery

Wide row ≈ **44px**. Dense row ≈ `12 + 20 + 6 + (16 × wrapped lines) + 6 + 16 + 12` ≈ **72–88px**
for a destination that wraps to 1–2 lines. At 24 rows that is a longer scroll — accepted, because
the alternative is a column a person cannot read, and the panel is open (i.e. the person is looking
at ONE connection) exactly when the dense shape applies.
