# v3.8 Milestone Audit — Document Intelligence, Automations & Connectors

**Status: `gaps_found`.** Not because the milestone did not ship — it did, and most of it is
traced wired end to end — but because one blocker, three partial requirements and five carried
debts must be recorded **before** the archive, not discovered after it.

---

## 1 · What this audit found FIRST, and why it matters most

⚠ **Seven of twelve phases had no `VERIFICATION.md` at all** — 201, 202, 203, 204, 205, 206, 209.
The phases shipped; the artefact was never written.

⚠ **`REQUIREMENTS.md` had not been touched since 2026-08-24.** `STATE-01`, `STATE-02`, `CONN-02`
and `CONN-03` read `Planned` in the traceability table **and** sat unchecked in the list above it,
after both their phases had shipped and after 206's round trip had been driven live in a browser.

Both are **fixed in this audit** (coverage now 12/12; all 11 requirements consistent in both
records). They are recorded here rather than quietly repaired because this is the **second
consecutive milestone** to close this way — v3.6's own retrospective reads *"the paperwork was the
problem, never the code"*, with five stale checkboxes and a phase absent from its checklist.

⭐ **And the cost is measurable rather than theoretical.** Phase 206 shipped **three** gaps that its
three inserts then existed to repair: an MCP connection could not be CREATED in the UI (206.1),
could not be BOUND to any step (206.2, `SEED-200`), and a workflow reaching outside could not
PUBLISH (206.3, `SEED-201`). A verification written at the time would very likely have caught the
binding gap. **206 is exactly the phase whose `VERIFICATION.md` was missing.**

## 2 · BLOCKER

### B-1 · The Settings edit panel offers a Check button on MCP connections that 500s — `CONN-02`

Two surfaces of one rule disagree, and only one was guarded.

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Row ⋯ menu — `ConnectionsTab.tsx:698` | ✅ `{onCheck && !isMcp && (` — correctly hidden |
| **Edit panel — `ConnectionFormPanel.tsx:1527`** | ⛔ renders the same control, and **`isMcp` appears nowhere in the file** |

Server side: `connectors.py:506-519` reads `capability` (which is `None` on an MCP row) and calls
`get_adapter(None)`; `registry.py:86-92` raises a **bare `KeyError`** for anything outside the
closed three-member set, and that `KeyError` is **not** in the route's `except` ladder → **HTTP 500**.

⚠ **`ConnectionsTab.tsx:1203-1206` states the intent and is falsified by its own code:**
*"passing them unconditionally keeps that decision in ONE place (the panel) rather than splitting
it across two files, which is how the two halves drift."* **The panel never took the decision.**

**Not a round-trip stopper** — flow 5 completes without pressing Check. But an operator has a
visible control that 500s, and no way to verify an MCP URL or token from Settings before running a
workflow. Independently re-derived by the auditor, not taken on the checker's word.

## 3 · PARTIAL requirements

### P-1 · `{{prior_run.*}}` reaches 3 of 7 executors — `STATE-01`, `STATE-02`

`_interpolate_prior_run_variables` + `_stateful_framing_block` are applied at exactly three sites in
`phase_types.py` — 702/706 (`llm_single`), 778/782 (`llm_agent`), 874/878 (`llm_batch_agents`).

⛔ **`_exec_llm_emit` composes its system prompt at :1377 with neither.** The file's own registry
docblock calls that path *"the SEALED FORCED EMIT — the only path that produces a typed
deliverable."* So on an `llm_emit` step a stateful workflow sends the model the **literal string**
`{{prior_run.output}}`, and STATE-02's `[NEW]/[UPDATED]/[RESOLVED]` framing never reaches the step
that actually writes the deliverable. `human_input.py:126` passes the raw prompt to a **human**.

**Why PARTIAL and not BLOCKER:** the UI only offers the chips on the three wired types
(`PhaseFormPanel.tsx` mounts `PromptVariableChips` at 1134/1192/1267 and nowhere in the `llm_emit`
or `llm_human_input` branches). UI and backend agree — but nothing stops an author typing the token
by hand, and it then **fails silently rather than loudly**.

### P-2 · Email thread identity is stored and read by nothing — `EML-02`

ROADMAP row 203 promises *"header metadata extracted, **thread deduplication**, and attachment
relationship links."*

- **Parsed:** `email_extraction_service.py:281-285, 350-352, 377-378, 411-413`
- **Stored:** `documents.py:1954-1959` → `email_message_id` / `email_in_reply_to` / `email_references`
- ⛔ **Consumed: nowhere.** A grep over `backend/app` for those three keys returns only the three
  write lines. No existence query on `email_message_id`; no `document_relationships` row from
  `in_reply_to` / `references` — only `rel_type='attached_to'` for attachments.

**So the same `.msg` uploaded twice creates two documents, and a reply chain produces N unrelated
documents.** The attachment half of EML-02 is genuinely wired; the thread half is not.

### P-3 · TAB-02 covers new ingests only

`backfill_document_table_chunks()` (`multimodal_service.py:407`) has **zero production callers** —
no route, no script, no admin action; its only other references are its own tests. Every
`document_tables` row created before Phase 202 therefore has no chunk in `document_chunks` and is
**invisible to semantic search**. (Re-embed does not rescue them: `reembed_service.py:158-200`
re-embeds from preserved `content`, so it preserves chunks that exist rather than creating missing
ones.)

## 4 · Warnings

**W-1 · Phase 209 is gated behind a flag whose cold default is `off`.**
`user_settings.py:1203` → `"visual_workflow_canvas": "off"`, described in its own comment as *"the
ONE authoritative cold default"*. `WorkflowBuilderPage.tsx:2234` passes the context
**spread-conditionally**: `{...(canvasEnabled ? { nameContext } : {})}`. With the flag at its
shipped default, `toolReadOnly` and `mcpServerUrls` are `undefined`, every `external_action` step
reads `CHANGES SOMETHING OUTSIDE` whatever the server declared, and the face degrades to a bare
`tool_name`.

⚠ **This qualifies THIS AUDIT'S OWN BROWSER EVIDENCE.** The 16/16 drive ran against a database
where the flag is `{"audience": "everyone"}` — flipped on by an operator. **It proves the behaviour
on a flag-flipped install, not a fresh one.** Fail-closed, so honest — but SC *"a read-only tool
stops claiming it changes something outside"* is only true after an operator flip.

**W-2 · The node face is absent from the RUN surface.** `WorkflowRunPage.tsx:985` calls
`nodeTitle(spec)` with **no** name context, so it falls to the bare tool name. `effectBannerFor`
appears only in `canvasModel.ts` and `PhaseSpineGraph.tsx` — the run page, `RunSpine.tsx`,
`RunStepList.tsx` and the panel's `PhaseCard` / `PhaseTimeline` render **no effect banner at all**.
The step that "says what it actually does" says it **in the builder only, not while it runs**. This
is `SEED-206`, planted hours before it was measured — now confirmed rather than suspected.

**W-3 · Blocking DNS inside an async handler.** `mcp_client.py:220` calls
`validate_mcp_destination` directly inside `async def _send_jsonrpc`; `egress.py:463` resolves via
`socket.getaddrinfo`. ⚠ **The sibling guard on the capability path is explicitly threadpooled for
exactly this reason** (`phase_types.py:2401`), and `CLAUDE.md`'s D-v2.5-01 forbids it. One rule,
two call sites, one wrapped.

**W-4 · A stale docblock that re-teaches the defect it caused.** `scheduler_service.py:68-75` still
asserts *"THE CAPS TRAVEL ON `inputs`… so the circuit breaker can read them"* — the exact belief
that produced the SCHED-02 silent-disarm. It is now false (they travel on `metadata` via
`arm_run_budget`), and the correction sits 80 lines below. **A reader who stops at the docstring
re-learns the bug.**

## 7 · Verdict and routing

**`gaps_found`.** Nothing here says a requirement is absent in code; it says **three are narrower
than their wording promises** (STATE-01/02 miss the deliverable step, EML-02 misses dedup, TAB-02
misses history) and **one shipped control 500s**.

**Recommended before archiving:**

1. **B-1** — a one-line `!isMcp` guard in `ConnectionFormPanel`, plus an MCP arm or a caught
   `KeyError` on the route. Small, and it is a user-visible 500.
2. **W-4** — delete the stale docblock. Free, and it currently teaches a defect.
3. **Record P-1, P-2, P-3, W-1, W-2, W-3 as accepted debt in the archive** with re-open triggers,
   rather than closing them silently. P-1 and P-2 belong in the next milestone as real work;
   W-1 is a launch decision, not a bug.

**Do NOT let the five carried debts disappear into the archive** — they are in this file's
frontmatter for exactly that reason.
