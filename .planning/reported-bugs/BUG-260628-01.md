# BUG-260628-01: Workflows page buries published workflows — drafts always at top regardless of filter

## What we observed

The Workflows page (`/workflows`) uses a project-based left-rail filter. Regardless of which project is selected (including "All projects"), **drafts are always sorted to the top** of the card grid. Published workflows are only visible after scrolling far down past all draft cards.

In a test environment with ~17 draft/seed workflows, the nearest published workflow appeared after 10+ scroll ticks (2+ full viewport heights). The page header says "Drafts are yours to shape; Published is live" but the layout contradicts this — live (published) workflows are harder to discover than draft ones.

## Why it matters

Users who want to **run** a workflow (the primary value path) have to scroll past all authoring-in-progress drafts to find runnable published workflows. "Run" buttons only appear on published cards; "Publish..." appears on drafts. The sort order optimizes for authoring discovery over execution discovery — the opposite of what the use case warrants.

## Hypothesized cause

The card grid likely sorts by `created_at` or `updated_at` descending, which places recently-edited drafts above older published ones. The `status` field (`draft` vs `published`) is not factored into the sort order.

## Surface classification

`Agentic-RAG` — this is a frontend layout/sort decision in the Workflows page component.
