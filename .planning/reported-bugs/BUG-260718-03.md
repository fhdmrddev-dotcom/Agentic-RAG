# BUG-260718-03: write_todos card still shows in the chat area (redundant with the workspace Todos panel)

## What we observed

The `write_todos` tool renders a card in the **chat area** (below the output/download-files cards) even though todos have their **own dedicated home in the right-side workspace panel** (the Todos section). The operator finds this redundant and visually unpolished — "what's the point of showing those cards in the chat when there's a workspace for it."

## Why it matters

Chat clutter: the same todo information appears in two places (chat seam card + workspace Todos panel). Below the output-files cards it reads as noise. Minor/cosmetic.

## Hypothesized cause (read-only check)

Phase 087-05 introduced a "seam" so the three panel-owned tools (`write_todos`, `workspace_write`, `ask_user`) render as a compact **SeamCard** pointer to the panel instead of a raw chat tool row:
- `frontend/src/components/chat/MessageItem.tsx:47-55` — `seamKindFor()` maps `write_todos`/`workspace_write`/`ask_user` to a seam kind.
- `frontend/src/components/chat/MessageItem.tsx:178-194` — the `write_todos` case derives the todo counts for the SeamCard.
- The dedicated home is `frontend/src/components/panel/TodosSection.tsx` inside `WorkspacePanel.tsx`.

So the SeamCard is **intentional** (a chat↔panel pointer), but the operator's feedback is that for todos specifically it's redundant with the always-present Todos panel and clutters the chat below the output files.

## Surface classification

`Agentic-RAG` — chat + workspace-panel surfaces. Routing candidate.

## Suggested routing

- **Fold into in-flight phase:** n/a (deferred)
- **Defer to future phase / milestone:** post-v3.3 chat/panel polish. Design decision — options: (a) suppress the `write_todos` seam card in chat entirely and rely on the Todos panel; (b) show the seam only when the panel is closed/collapsed; (c) make it a single quiet inline line instead of a card. Needs a design call (sketch), not a blind removal — the seam was a deliberate chat↔panel affordance.
