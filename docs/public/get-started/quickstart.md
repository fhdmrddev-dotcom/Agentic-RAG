This guide takes you from an empty Library to a cited answer. You need a Syrel account (see [Sign in and accept an invitation](/docs/get-started/sign-in)) and one document you are happy to upload, such as a PDF or a Word file.

## 1. Add a document

1. Open **Library** from the rail on the left.
2. On the **Documents** tab, pick a folder you own in the folder tree, or stay at the top level.
3. Drop your file on the upload area, or click it and choose a file. Uploads can be up to 50 MB.

Syrel reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and CSV files, plain text, Markdown, HTML, EPUB, email files (`.eml`, `.msg`), common image formats and `.dxf` drawings.

## 2. Wait until it is ready

While Syrel processes the file, its row shows how far it has got: reading the file, reading tables, reading images, splitting into sections, making it searchable and reading document details. Stages that do not apply to your file are struck through. When the row says **Ready**, the document is indexed and searchable.

> **Note:** If a document fails, its row names the stage that failed. You can re-ingest it from the row once the cause is fixed.

## 3. Ask a question

1. Open **Chat** from the rail and start a new chat.
2. Type a question your document can answer, for example "What are the payment terms in this contract?", and press Enter.

You can watch Syrel work while it answers: it shows what it is doing in plain words ("Searching knowledge base…") and each tool it uses as a card you can open.

## 4. Check the citations

- Each claim taken from your documents carries a numbered marker such as `[1]`. Hover a marker to preview the passage; click it to pin the passage and highlight it in the references under the answer.
- The answer carries a confidence badge: **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. A low-confidence answer asks you to check the sources.
- A sentence without a marker is the model's general knowledge, not something it found in your documents.
