You reach Syrel through your organisation's address for it. The sign-in screen asks for your email first and decides the next step from it.

## Signing in

1. Enter your work email and choose **Continue**.
2. If your company has set up single sign-on for your email domain, Syrel sends you to your company's sign-in page. After you sign in there, you come back to Syrel.
3. Otherwise the password field appears. Enter your password to sign in.

If you know your company uses single sign-on but you were not redirected, enter your email and choose **Sign in with SSO**.

Password sign-in always stays available as a fallback, even when single sign-on is set up.

## Creating an account

From the sign-in screen you can switch to creating a new account with an email and a password. A new account starts in its own personal organisation. What that organisation may do depends on the plan an operator assigns to it — see [Plans and tiers](/docs/administer/org/plans-and-tiers).

## Accepting an invitation

An organisation admin can invite you by email or by sharing a link. Open the link and:

- If you are not signed in, sign in or create an account from the invitation screen. You join the organisation as soon as you are signed in.
- If you are already signed in, you join straight away. If you already belong to that organisation, Syrel says so and takes you into the app.
- If the invitation has expired or been revoked, Syrel tells you to ask whoever invited you to resend it.

The invitation decides your role: **Member** or **Org admin**. See [Organisations and what is shared](/docs/get-started/workspaces-and-orgs) for what each role can do.

> **Note:** Until an operator sets up an email provider, a deployment does not send invitation emails. Your admin can still copy the invitation link and send it to you.

## If your account is disabled

A platform operator can disable an account. A disabled account cannot use Syrel, and the app tells you to contact your administrator.
