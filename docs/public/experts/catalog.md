Open **Experts** from the rail to see the catalog. It lists only the Experts available to you; search by name, topic or capability.

![The Experts catalog with Expert cards](/docs-assets/shots/experts-catalog.png)

## An Expert's card and details

Each card says what the Expert is for. Open it to see its details:

- **When to summon this Expert** — the kind of question it is built for.
- **Bound folders** and **knowledge composition** — the documents it answers from.
- **Bound skills** — the instructions it brings.
- **One-click action prompts** — starter questions you can send straight away.
- **Sample deliverable output** — what a typical result looks like.

## Starting a chat

Choose **Start Chat** to open a new chat scoped to that Expert, with its starter prompts ready to use. You can also bring an Expert into a chat you already have — see [Bringing an Expert into a chat](/docs/experts/using-experts).

## When an Expert is not ready

The catalog tells you why an Expert cannot help yet, instead of letting it fail mid-answer:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Requires** a service — **not connected** | The Expert needs a connection your organisation has not set up. The card offers to connect it when you are allowed to. |
| An admin must install it | Its documents are not in your organisation yet. An org admin installs it from the catalog. |
| Installing… / Still installing | Its documents are being copied and indexed. It can join a chat once they are ready. |
| Install failed | An org admin can retry the install. |

## Why the catalog may be empty

Which Experts your organisation can see depends on its plan; the starter Experts are available to organisations on the enterprise tier. If your organisation's plan does not include Experts, the catalog says so rather than showing an empty page. Ask your administrator.
