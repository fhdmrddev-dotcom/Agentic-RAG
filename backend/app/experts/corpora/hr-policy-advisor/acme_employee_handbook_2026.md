# ACME Corporation - Employee Handbook 2026

## Scope

This handbook applies to all ACME Corporation employees on a permanent employment contract. Contractors and agency workers are covered by the terms of their own agreements. Where local law grants a more generous entitlement, local law prevails.

## Leave and Time Off

| Entitlement | Full-time employees | Part-time employees |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Annual paid time off (full-time) | 23 days | Pro rata to contracted hours |
| Paid parental leave | 18 weeks | 18 weeks at pro rata pay |
| Paid sick leave | 10 days per year | Pro rata to contracted hours |
| Bereavement leave | 5 days per event | 5 days per event |
| Volunteering leave | 2 days per year | 1 day per year |

## Eligibility

- Annual paid time off accrues monthly from the first day of employment.
- Paid parental leave is available to every employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption, or surrogacy, after completing 6 months of continuous service. It may be taken in one block or in up to three blocks within 12 months of the child's arrival.
- Paid sick leave does not carry over from one calendar year to the next.

## Carry-Over

Employees may carry up to 5 unused days of annual paid time off into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of that year or they lapse. Payment in lieu of unused time off is made only on leaving the company.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays are in addition to annual paid time off. ACME observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is contracted. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off within the following two months.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

| Role category | Minimum office days per week | Fully remote permitted |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Engineering and product | 2 | By manager approval |
| Manufacturing and quality | 5 | No |
| Sales and customer success | 1 | By manager approval |
| Corporate functions | 2 | By manager approval |

Employees working remotely must use a company-managed laptop, connect through the corporate VPN, and work from the country in which they are contracted. Working from another country for more than 20 working days in a calendar year requires prior approval from People Operations.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours for full-time employees. Flexible start and finish times are permitted between 07:00 and 19:00 local time, provided core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 are kept.
