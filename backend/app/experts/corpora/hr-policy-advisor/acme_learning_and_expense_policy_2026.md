# ACME Corporation - Learning, Development and Expense Policy 2026

## Learning and Development Benefits

| Benefit | Amount or limit | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Annual learning stipend | $1,850 | Per employee per calendar year, not carried over |
| Professional certification exam fees | Paid in full | Two attempts per certification |
| Conference attendance | One external conference per year | Manager approval required |
| Tuition assistance (degree programmes) | Up to $7,500 per year | Subject to a 12-month retention agreement |
| Internal mentoring programme | Open to all employees | Enrolment each January and July |

The annual learning stipend may be spent on courses, books, online learning subscriptions, and workshop fees that relate to the employee's current role or an agreed development plan. Unspent stipend does not roll into the next calendar year.

## Travel and Expense Limits

| Expense category | Limit | Approval |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Domestic per diem (meals and incidentals) | $75 per day | Automatic |
| International per diem (meals and incidentals) | $95 per day | Automatic |
| Hotel - standard city | $220 per night | Automatic |
| Hotel - high-cost city (New York, London, Tokyo, Singapore) | $340 per night | Automatic |
| Economy airfare | Lowest logical fare | Automatic |
| Premium economy airfare | Flights over 8 hours | Manager |
| Business class airfare | Flights over 11 hours | Vice President |
| Client entertainment | $150 per attendee | Manager |

## Submitting Expenses

- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred, with an itemised receipt for any single item above $25.
- Corporate card holders must reconcile statements by the 10th of each month.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of approved client entertainment.
- Personal travel combined with business travel is permitted only when the business portion of the fare is not increased.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Traffic and parking fines, personal grooming, in-room entertainment, loyalty-programme fees, and upgrades purchased without the required approval are not reimbursable.

## Policy Owner

This policy is owned by the Finance department together with People Operations and is reviewed every twelve months.
