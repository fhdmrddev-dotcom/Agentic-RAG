# ACME Corporation - Tier-1 Supplier Scorecard, Q3 2026

ACME Corporation sources its specialized semiconductor packaging and optical sub-assemblies from three Tier-1 suppliers in Southeast Asia. This scorecard is prepared by Supply Chain Operations each quarter and reviewed with the suppliers at the quarterly business review.

## Scoring Method

Each supplier is scored on four weighted measures: supplier on-time delivery (40%), quality in defects per million (30%), lead-time stability (20%), and responsiveness to engineering change requests (10%). A score of A requires at least 95% on-time delivery and fewer than 200 defects per million.

## Commentary

**Halvorsen Optics** remains the top supplier by spend and the only supplier rated A. Its concentration of spend is above ACME's internal single-supplier guideline, and a second qualified source for optical sub-assemblies is being evaluated.

**Tanjong Packaging** has the longest lead time of the three. Two late shipments in August were caused by a substrate shortage at its own sub-supplier. Tanjong is on a corrective action plan with a target of 94% on-time delivery by the end of Q4 2026.

**Binh Minh Microsystems** completed qualification of a second production line in July, which is expected to shorten its lead time by about five days from Q1 2027.

## Risk Notes

All three Tier-1 suppliers operate in Southeast Asia. Supply Chain Operations maintains safety stock for the highest-risk parts and reviews regional logistics disruption alerts weekly. Dual-sourcing for optical sub-assemblies is the top supply-chain priority for the next two quarters.
