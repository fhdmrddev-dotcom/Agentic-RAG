## Commentary

Finished-goods stock-outs were concentrated in two optical module SKUs that depend on sub-assemblies from the top Tier-1 supplier. Safety stock for those SKUs was raised from four to six weeks of cover in September. The backorder rate fell for the second consecutive quarter and is now within target.

## Actions for Q4 2026

- Extend the Reno pick-and-pack process to the Columbus site by November.
- Review carrier performance in Columbus after the September carrier change.
- Reduce service and spare parts cover toward the 10-week policy level by clearing slow-moving stock.
