## Indemnification

Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims arising from its own gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or breach of applicable law. Kestrel Freight Partners additionally indemnifies ACME against customs penalties caused by misclassification of goods where the classification was prepared by Kestrel staff. The indemnities for bodily injury, fraud, and breach of confidentiality sit outside the aggregate liability cap.

## Data Protection

Kestrel Freight Partners processes shipment manifests, consignee contact details, and commercial invoices on ACME's behalf as a processor. Shipment data must be stored in data centres located in the United States or the European Economic Area. Kestrel must report any personal-data breach affecting ACME records to ACME's privacy office within 48 hours of discovery and must support ACME's own regulatory notifications.

## Service Levels and Credits

| Service level | Target | Credit if missed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Customs clearance within 2 business days | 97% of entries per month | 3% of the monthly brokerage fee |
| Shipment status update within 4 hours of milestone | 99% of milestones per month | 2% of the monthly forwarding fee |
| Damage or loss claims acknowledged | Within 5 business days | 1% of the monthly forwarding fee |

Service credits are capped at 12% of the fees for the affected month and are ACME's sole financial remedy for a missed service level, without prejudice to termination rights for repeated failure. Three missed service levels in any rolling six-month period constitute a material breach.

## Assignment and Subcontracting

Neither party may assign the agreement without prior written consent, except to an affiliate acquiring substantially all of the relevant business. Kestrel may subcontract local trucking and port handling but remains fully responsible for subcontractor performance.
